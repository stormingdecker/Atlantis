Helen's Stronghold, Late Bronze Age

Status: Draft. Stage-4 boss-sequence candidate — the "confront the seductive face of EPOCH" climax. See the Missions Overview, Helen, and the Helen's Descendants artifact.

Premise

The Bronze-Age Aegean is ATA's exclusive zone — and EPOCH still put a queen in it. Helen is EPOCH's premier deep-reach embed: a heavily Far-Traveler-invested glamour-psi asset, a "Grade-III generalist" whose reach is the threat, not her sword-arm. Getting her here at all cost EPOCH a fortune; the points came out of her combat and durability.

Her stronghold is the seat of her influence network — a citadel where the local powers are already enthralled, where charisma is a garrison. The team doesn't storm a fortress so much as walk into a court that has been taught to love its captor. Every guard, courtier, and king in the walls will fight for Helen without her asking. This is a candidate boss location, so it escalates to full painterly boss art (per Overview) — the map itself signals this fight matters.

Because she sits deep in EPOCH's reachable-window infrastructure, her drop-home is cheap. Corner her badly and she leaves clean unless the team can jam her anchor. Killing her is the easy, worst-long-term outcome; capturing her is arc-shaping.

Map & Setting

A multi-tile approach-to-throne progression (per top-down VTT conventions), escalating in art density as the team nears her.

  • Outer — the Enthralled Town. The citadel's lower town: agora, docks or gate, packed streets of devotees. Exploration + social/period-cover pillar. Bronze-Age Aegean at a glance — cyclopean masonry, painted plaster, 10-ft exterior grid. The danger is social, not martial: raise an alarm and the whole population turns.
  • Middle — the Palace Court. Megaron approaches, colonnaded courts, guest halls full of devotees and decoys. Puzzle + social pillar. Ships lit (open court) and dark (night infiltration). This is where the glamour thickens and the "which one is really Helen?" problem lives.
  • Inner — the Throne Megaron. The great hearth-hall, painted floor-to-rafter, her seat at its heart. Combat + climax pillar. Full boss-art treatment: heavier texture, richer light, the same 90° top-down rules — only the detail density rises.

Objectives

  1. Get in past an enthralled populace without tripping the social alarm that turns the town into her army.
  2. Resist or turn the glamour — hold Will against the field, and ideally weaponize a devotee or two against her.
  3. Find the real Helen among decoys, doubles, and true believers positioned to soak first contact.
  4. Jam her anchor so a cornered Helen can't simply drop home — the precondition for any capture.
  5. Resolve her: capture (campaign-shaping — intel, biotech sample, leverage), or kill (easy, worst long-term, ripple-risky).

Capture-over-kill is heavily incentivized. She is a Mark VI+ intelligence trove and a live glamour-psi bloodline sample; a corpse is a squandered arc and a possible timeline catastrophe.

Encounters & Beats

Exploration — the Approach (social infiltration).

  • The town loves her. Cover-work, not stealth, gets the team inside — pass as suppliants, traders, or an embassy.
  • Every friendly NPC is a potential watcher; a single blown interaction summons the enthralled crowd, which is the real "wall."
  • Trained players note their own agent-sense pinging — she felt them enter her range and has positioned pieces preemptively.

Puzzle — the Glamour & the Decoys.

  • Inside the court the psi-glamour is always on: PCs make ongoing Will checks, failure biasing them toward sympathy and hesitation.
  • She has surrounded herself with beautiful doubles and fervent devotees, and the field makes every candidate seem "impossibly her."
  • Reading the augmentation as psi (which agents can) is the key to picking the real target — and, better, to turning a devotee's faith into a lever against her.

Combat — the Throne (boss sequence).

  • A punishing-but-survivable confrontation. Helen herself is a soft combatant who never fights straight.
  • The fight is her court: charmed household guard, a charmed local king throwing bodies in the way, bystanders manufacturing cover for her exfil.
  • The team must fight through love to reach her while the glamour saps their resolve, and close the anchor-jam before she drops home. Winning is reaching her, not out-dueling her.

Open Design Questions

  • Location. Two strong options: a Troy-adjacent citadel (leans into the canon Trojan War and Hypotheses A/B on Helen's page) versus a hidden Aegean/Anatolian stronghold (a bespoke seat of her network, freeing the mission from Iliad timing). Recommend: a hidden Anatolian citadel pre-war, so capturing her doesn't require un-writing the Trojan War's M10+ ripple.
  • Does she know she's an agent? Her self-awareness changes every conversation — true believer in her own cover vs. cold operative. Ties directly to Helen.md's open question.
  • The anchor-jam mechanic. Skill challenge, consumable device, or a fixed map objective (destroy the anchor cache)? Recommend: a physical anchor node on the throne tile, so combat and objective share a space.
  • Decoy count and tells. How many doubles, and what reliable tell (agent-sense, a physical marker, behavioral) lets a sharp team cut through fairly?
  • Aftermath if captured. A captured Helen is a full follow-on arc — where is she held, and does EPOCH mount a recovery? (See Overview staging note: Helen as Act II climax, the 2200s facility as Act III finale.)
