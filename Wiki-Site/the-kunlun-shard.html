The Kunlun Shard

EPOCH's gradient anchor. A second fragment of the multi-dimensional meteor that created The Origin — but this one impacted in our future.

What It Is

A smaller piece of the original multi-dimensional meteor that struck Earth in the 2200s, in the high Tibetan plateau (Kunlun mountains region). The dominant world power of that era — the Russian-Chinese hegemony that has consolidated across the early 2200s — discovered it within years of impact, recognized its parachronic properties (their physics had already matured to the point where the discovery was expected rather than serendipitous), and formed EPOCH around it.

EPOCH's headquarters, Shambhala, is built directly on the impact site in the 2200s. The agency's operational range extends both forward (deeper into their own future) and backward — into the player team's present, antiquity, and the deep historical past. ATA, operating from our present, only became aware of EPOCH's existence in the 20th century when EPOCH's backward-projected agents began operating openly enough to be detected.

Why It Arrived Late

The "multi-dimensional" property of the source meteor is what produces this temporal displacement. Fragments ejected during the original Origin's atmospheric entry do not all follow conventional trajectories — some travel through dimensions parallel to ours before re-entering normal spacetime at displaced spatial and temporal coordinates. The Kunlun fragment took approximately 6200 years longer than the Origin to "arrive."

A working hypothesis (not confirmed canon — see ../GM-Secrets/): the displacement is not random. The shards may be seeded at moments of civilizational inflection — the Origin at the dawn of complex civilization, the Kunlun at a moment of late-modern transition. If true, the pattern of shard arrivals is intentional, which raises the question of whose intent.

Comparison to the Origin

The Kunlun Shard shares the original meteor's essential properties:

  • Emits constant heat and characteristic radiance from the same form of radioactive decay.
  • Anchors a temporal gradient that allows time travel along its length.
  • Powers an independent Chronograph instrument capable of detecting timeline ripples.
  • Has never been depleted in observation.

It differs from the Origin shard in three operationally significant ways:

  1. Smaller. Approximately one-eighth the mass of the Origin meteor. Its gradient's maximum reach is shorter — roughly 4500 years in either temporal direction from its impact moment, versus the Origin's roughly 6000.
  2. Located in the future. Anchored at ~2200 CE. This is the defining structural fact about EPOCH.
  3. Subtly different resonance. The shard's "frequency" diverges slightly from the original Origin's, producing measurably different Chronograph readings of the same events.

Operational Window

  • Earliest reachable point (from Kunlun): ~2300 BCE.
  • Anchor point: ~2200 CE.
  • Latest reachable point: ~6700 CE.

This gives EPOCH access to:

  • Cheap travel to the late modern era, the players' present, and the 2200s future (close to the anchor).
  • Moderate-cost travel to the Roman, Medieval, Renaissance, and Early Modern eras.
  • Expensive travel to the Bronze Age (2300–1200 BCE), within range but at the edge of practical operations.
  • No access to the deep Bronze Age (4000–2300 BCE). ATA's exclusive domain.

EPOCH's home turf is the 1900s–2200s CE. They operate there with overwhelming cost advantage. ATA's modern-era operations are correspondingly more expensive and more cautious.

The Shambhala Mythology

Tibetan Buddhism's tradition of Shambhala — a hidden kingdom of enlightened beings preserving secret knowledge — is conspicuously aligned with the eventual EPOCH facility. The mythology pre-dates EPOCH's actual formation by roughly two thousand years.

Two hypotheses, both interesting:

  1. Coincidence + opportunism. The Buddhist Shambhala myth has independent origins (rooted in Kalachakra eschatology). EPOCH simply exploited the existing mythology when building their cover. The facility was named because the cover was already there.
  2. Retroactive seeding. EPOCH agents operating in our past (500 BCE – 1500 CE Tibet) deliberately seeded the Shambhala mythology over centuries, knowing they would need cultural cover for their future facility. The myth is EPOCH propaganda planted retroactively. The self-fulfilling temporal loop makes EPOCH's existence feel inevitable to anyone who studies the local tradition.

EPOCH internal records favor hypothesis 2. ATA Reality Analysis favors hypothesis 1 — because hypothesis 2 implies EPOCH was operating in our past for centuries before ATA detected them, which is terrifying.

EPOCH's Deep-Past Foothold — the Tarim Basin

The shard's ~2200 CE impact is on the high Tibetan plateau, but EPOCH's presence in the deep past clusters just north of it, in the habitable Tarim Basin below the Kunlun range's wall. The plateau itself is brutal; the basin's string of oases is where a backward-projected embed community could actually live. This is where EPOCH first put down roots in antiquity — near their anchor's spatial coordinate, at the very edge of their gradient reach (~2300 BCE) — and it leaves a real archaeological fingerprint: the Tarim mummies, a naturally-preserved, culturally-cosmopolitan population that has long puzzled researchers for seeming out of place. In-setting, they are EPOCH's oldest footprint on Earth — the ground truth behind the Shambhala-seeding operations, and a deep-past investigation site of enormous lore value.

(Note the geography is a deliberate liberty: impact on the plateau, settlement in the adjacent basin. The two are neighbors, separated by the Kunlun range.)

The 1957 Incident — Retconned

For decades, ATA central command believed EPOCH was founded around 1957–1962 by a Russian-Chinese joint intelligence project that received an ATA defector with parachronic theory. This belief is wrong.

The "defector" was an EPOCH agent projected backward from the 2200s, posing as a defecting ATA temporal physicist. His mission was to establish the modern-era cover identity for EPOCH (the "Russian-Chinese joint intelligence project" framing) and to seed the 1957 narrative that ATA central command would believe for the next 70 years. The mission succeeded completely. ATA's intelligence files on EPOCH's origin are almost entirely fabricated, planted by EPOCH at their own creation.

The truth — that EPOCH is a future agency, not a 1950s one — is one of the campaign's most consequential late-game reveals. When players learn this, the entire shape of the conflict changes. Every prior assumption about EPOCH's resources, leadership, and motivations is recontextualized.

First Contact (In Campaign)

The players' first thread to the Kunlun Shard is small and easy to miss: a location hint recovered from the EPOCH prisoner captured at the end of Arc 00. Analysis of the operative's kit surfaces coordinates that mean nothing to fresh recruits — a place, and a time, they can neither reach nor interpret yet. It is the seed of everything above: much of the campaign is the players closing the gap between that fragment and the full truth — that the enemy's anchor sits in the future — which lands as one of the biggest late-game reveals (see the 1957 retcon).

Status

  • Kunlun Shard confirmed canon as EPOCH's gradient anchor.
  • Impact date: ~2200 CE.
  • Shambhala facility built in the 2200s at impact site.
  • EPOCH formation: in the 2200s Russian-Chinese hegemony, post-impact. Exact founding date open.
  • The Bronze Age (4000–2300 BCE) is ATA's exclusive domain for active operations.
  • The 1957 defector was an EPOCH agent establishing modern-era cover. Late-campaign reveal.
