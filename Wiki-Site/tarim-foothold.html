The Tarim Foothold — ~1900–1930s CE / ~2000 BCE

Status: Draft. Full build-out of the Tarim mummies seed — EPOCH's oldest footprint on Earth. See the Missions Overview and The Kunlun Shard § Deep-Past Foothold.

Premise

The Tarim Basin, in the Taklamakan desert of what is now northwest China, is where EPOCH first put down roots in human history — a backward-projected embed community settled at the very edge of their gradient's reach (~2300 BCE), on the habitable ground nearest their future anchor's spatial coordinate. The Kunlun Shard does not "arrive" until the ~2200s, yet its people were already here, four thousand years earlier. The desert's arid salt preserved them: the real Tarim mummies — cosmopolitan, felt-hatted, tartan-clad, and long "out of place" to archaeologists — are that fingerprint.

This is the mission that lands the campaign's most chilling deep-history truth: EPOCH was operating in humanity's past centuries before ATA believed it existed — the ground behind the Shambhala-seeding hypothesis and, downstream, the 1957 retcon. The prize is physical: a preserved EPOCH operative or their kit — a biological/tech artifact of enormous value.

Two framings; run (A) first, hold (B) for a high-investment follow-up.

  • (A) Archaeology-era insert (~1900–1930s) — recommended entry. The era of the real Silk Road expeditions (Sven Hedin, Aurel Stein) opening Loulan and Xiaohe. ATA embeds under expedition cover to read the anomalous burials before mundane science misfiles them or EPOCH sanitizes the record — recover a preserved embed body or leaked Mark-tech and grasp how deep EPOCH's past runs. Accessible era, espionage-and-excavation flavor, a rival party in the sand.
  • (B) Deep-Bronze-Age insert (~2000 BCE) — later, high-cost. Drop into the living oasis settlement itself: deep in ATA's exclusive domain, but on EPOCH's spatial home ground — a rare, tense reach demanding a heavily Far-Traveler-invested team. Witness the foothold in operation.

Map & Setting

Two stacked/linked play surfaces (per top-down VTT conventions); the team crosses from one to the other. Unmistakably NW-China desert at a glance — the Taklamakan is a lethal environment, a pillar in its own right.

  • Upper — the Desert Approach / Oasis. Windward dunes, dry riverbeds, a poplar-fringed oasis, and (framing A) the canvas-and-camel expedition camp. Exploration + tension pillar. 30-ft wilderness scale on the approach, 10-ft at the camp/oasis. Sand and salt-crust floors; sun baked from the northwest.
  • Lower — the Excavation / Tomb Interior. (A) An opened dig cutting and the sealed burial beneath — timber-framed shaft graves, boat-shaped coffins, poled markers. (B) the settlement's mortuary chamber, still tended. Puzzle + combat pillar. Ships in lit and dark (dust-storm blackout / lamps-out infiltration) layers.

Objectives

  1. Establish cover and reach the anomalous burials — expedition credentials (A) or oasis infiltration (B) — before the window closes.
  2. Read the site. Decipher the burial and preservation methods: what is period-correct desiccation, and what is quietly engineered?
  3. Recover the prize — a preserved EPOCH operative's body and/or leaked Mark-tier kit, the biological/tech artifact that proves the foothold.
  4. Confirm the timeline. Establish that EPOCH's presence predates ATA's earliest estimate by millennia — evidence tying the basin to Shambhala-seeding.
  5. Control the record and extract. Deny the truth to mundane science and to any EPOCH cleanup — decide what the desert is allowed to keep — then drop home heavy.

Capture-and-recover is the whole point: a preserved operative is a peerless sample for the Genetics Lab, and the dating evidence reshapes ATA's threat model. Letting EPOCH sanitize the site first is the failure state.

Encounters & Beats

Exploration — the Desert & the Dig.

  • Insertion into a hostile environment: heat, thirst, dust storms, and the long walk in. The desert is an antagonist before any NPC is.
  • (A) Work the expedition: earn access to the cutting past a proprietary lead archaeologist; (B) move unseen through a functioning oasis where every settler is a potential watcher — EPOCH's spatial home turf.
  • The first itch: a Chronograph flutter over graves that shouldn't register, and grave goods a shade too cosmopolitan for the era.

Puzzle — Reading the Burial.

  • Diagnose the preservation: separate natural salt-desiccation from deliberate, era-inappropriate technique. The tell is intent — someone preserved these bodies to be found, or to last.
  • Decode what the site is: a founding-community cemetery, its material culture the residue of people equipped from a future they never named. Piece the dating together into the reveal.

Combat / Tension — the Sanitization.

  • The core threat is an EPOCH cleanup team racing to erase the record — subtle, far-traveler-light in framing (A) where EPOCH's reach into the early 20th c. is cheap, or a rare hardened embed cadre in framing (B). The fight is over the artifact and the record, on your terms (chokepoints, the storm, the shaft) rather than an open firefight.
  • Framing (A) alternative/addition: a rival mundane expedition — no supernatural knowledge, just competitors who will publish or plunder first, complicating cover without a kill-box.
  • The desert itself can force the climax: a burying storm that closes the window and pins both sides in the cutting.

Open Design Questions

  • How much lore to spend here. Full "EPOCH was in the deep past before it existed" reveal in framing (A), or seed it in (A) and pay it off in the Bronze-Age drop (B)? (Mirrors the Historical Anomalies open question.)
  • The prize's state. Intact preserved operative (biological jackpot, ripple-heavy to remove) vs. leaked Mark-tech only vs. a body already half-sanitized by EPOCH — how much do the players actually get out?
  • EPOCH presence in framing (A). Live cleanup team on-site (more action) vs. chilling aftermath of a record already scrubbed (more dread). Recommend a live but subtle team with one near-miss.
  • Rival-expedition identity. Fictionalized Hedin/Stein analogue, or a named original? How much real Silk Road history to invoke without over-anchoring canon.
  • Framing (B) cost & tie-in. What Far-Traveler investment gates the Bronze-Age drop, and does it slot into a later high-stakes op the way (A) slots into The Long 1910s?
  • Extraction signature & Reality Log. Does erasing/preserving the record here leave a diagnostic tell readable against the Reality Log, shared with other anomaly missions?
