Bronze Age

The golden window for agency operations. Roughly 3300–1200 BCE.

Why It Matters

The Bronze Age sits close to The Origin — temporal travel here is cheap, gradient navigation is forgiving, and the era is full of formative civilizations (Minoan, Mycenaean, Egyptian, Hittite, Akkadian) whose decisions ripple forward across the entire downstream timeline.

If you wanted to invest one mission and watch it pay dividends for three thousand years, you do it here.

Major Operations

  • The Minotaur Program (../Myth-Engineering/Minotaur-Program.md) — Cretan deployment, mid-Bronze Age.
  • The Anubis Initiative (../Myth-Engineering/Anubis-Initiative.md) — Egyptian deployment, early-to-mid Bronze Age.
  • The Trojan Operation — both agencies attribute, neither admits. Helen of Troy is the named embedded agent. The war itself was cover for a much larger contest over a Bronze Age fuel cache rumored to have been moved through the city. (Speculative — needs expansion.)

Confirmed Embedded Agents (working list)

  • Helen — EPOCH visible embed. Glamour-psi. Catalyst for the Trojan War. Boss-tier encounter.
  • Theseus — short-contract op, ATA, extraction after Minotaur disposal.
  • Hatshepsut — long-embed, ATA. Her unusual reign is the agency's most successful direct-rule operation.

See ../Embedded-Agents/Overview.md for the full embed roster and the "EPOCH Power Curve" doctrine that shapes early-campaign encounters with their agents.

The Bronze Age Collapse and the Disruption

Both agencies treat the ~1200 BCE collapse as a catastrophic operational failure. Eight major civilizations destabilize or fall in under a century. Neither agency claims to have caused it. Both have theories about who did.

For ATA specifically, the collapse coincides with the Disruption Event — the end of the Bronze Age surface-cover era of Atlantis operations, after which the facility went fully underwater-only. See ../GM-Secrets/Atlantis-Fall.md for the full hypotheses. The two may be the same event, or causally linked, or coincidental — each reading has different implications for the campaign.
