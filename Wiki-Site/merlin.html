Merlin

ATA embed in post-Roman Britain, seated as Arthur's advisor to counter EPOCH's god-king play. The "wizard" of Camelot — and, once the players work it out, their secret ally in the deep past.

  • Faction: ATA. Long-embed visible operative.
  • Cover: Merlin — counsellor, prophet, and sorcerer to Arthur's court.
  • Role in the arc: the ATA counter-move to the Lady of the Lake's EPOCH operation; the party's likely ally in the Camelot Arc.

Why he reads as a wizard

Every "impossible" thing about Merlin is a plain description of an infused ATA agent, distorted by a 6th-century court with no other vocabulary:

  • Prophecy. Memory-privilege + Reality-Log briefing + (likely) natural precog. Merlin "sees the future" because he was briefed on it and can feel the timeline's shape — the same gift that made Sigerson precious.
  • Agelessness. Slow aging from long infusion. An advisor who serves across generations "cannot be aging normally" — so the folk read him as living backward through time.
  • "Magic." Agency kit and psi, uncategorizable to the era: light without fire, healing beyond a leech's craft, a word that bends a wavering lord. Advanced tech reframed as sorcery.
  • He glows faintly in the dark. Infusion luminescence — carefully hidden, occasionally seen, feeding the legend.

The long game

Merlin's mission is not the players' smash-and-grab. It is strategic: keep post-Roman Britain from calcifying into the shape EPOCH wants — steer Arthur toward a durable, EPOCH-unfriendly settlement; contest the Lady without blowing either cover; deny EPOCH its stability anchor. He has invested years in Arthur.

This makes him a complicated ally:

  • He reads as ATA to agent-sense — the players will feel a friend in the room before they know who. The reveal ("the terrifying wizard is one of us") is a great beat.
  • His goals overlap the team's but aren't identical. Merlin may not want Excalibur simply extracted if the grab wrecks his decade of court-shaping — or he may see handing it to ATA as the cleaner end. Negotiating that is the arc's diplomacy.
  • He knows the board cold: who the Lady is, what Arthur is, where the sword sits, what's in the Lake. He is the team's shortcut through Stage I — if they earn his trust.

Mechanical notes

  • Senior NPC / quest-giver, not a PC — a high-value ally and information broker, in the Sigerson/senior-agent tier (natural precog + long service). Not a combat piece; his power is knowledge and influence.
  • Agent-sense: reads clearly as ATA-Origin infusion at close range — the reliable tell that separates him from the Kunlun-signature Lady.
  • The disappearance. Merlin's legendary vanishing (imprisoned in a tree/cave/crystal by Nimue) reads as an extraction or a burial-chamber pause — a hook for a later mission, or the ATA exfil that ends his Britain tour.

Open questions

  • Is Merlin a named ATA figure the players meet again elsewhere/elsewhen, or Britain-only? (A recurring deep-past contact is the richer choice.)
  • Nimue. The "Merlin's downfall" figure — an EPOCH counter-agent who neutralizes him (tying to the Lady's faction), or an ATA successor who relieves him? Decides how his tour ends.
  • How much does he tell the players? A forthcoming ally shortcuts the arc; a cagey one (protecting his long game) makes them earn it. Recommend cagey-then-warming.
