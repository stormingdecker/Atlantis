The Origin Vault

Status: Draft. The Stage-1 recruitment/training/reveal tutorial. See the Missions Overview, the Atlantis Facility, and The Origin.
Naming note (resolving the audit's HQ ambiguity): the Atlantis facility is ATA's headquarters, built around the Origin meteor's crater. "The Origin Vault" is the innermost chamber that houses the Origin shard itself. This mission is the recruit's first descent into the facility, ending at that chamber.

Premise

The onboarding tutorial and the campaign's foundational awe-beat. Freshly-recruited, freshly-infused agents get their first guided descent into the Atlantis facility — a safe, low-stakes tour that teaches the core loop (brief → transit → operate → return) and the facility's systems, and ends standing before the glowing Origin shard while a senior agent tells them what they've actually signed up for.

No enemy, no failure state. The "mission" is learning the game and feeling the weight of it.

Map & Setting

The Atlantis facility ring (the same top-down annular layout the projector app renders): an inner ring of infrastructure sectors around the central Origin crater, an outer ring of support sectors. See Atlantis-Facility.md and Base-Upgrades.md for the sectors. The master map is the ring; a handful of rooms are detail play-tiles:

  • Command Center (I-1) — the briefing room. Where the tour and the Chronograph readout are introduced.
  • A training / simulation chamber — the safe practice space (Museum sim chambers at high tier; the Commons gym / Medical combat-rehab at low tier).
  • The Transit Hub (I-8) — where a live boost/drop demo teaches the gradient's asymmetry.
  • The Genetics Lab (I-6) / Medical (O-6) — infusion and gear issue.
  • The Origin Vault — the innermost meteor chamber: the vast warm crater, the softly-glowing shard at its heart. The reveal. Full painterly boss-sequence art treatment (per Overview) to signal this matters — even though nothing is fought here.

Objectives

Framed as onboarding rather than win/lose:

  1. Orientation tour — learn the sector system and the ring layout.
  2. Meet the leadership — Chief (recurring mission-giver) and Doc (infusion).
  3. Run a training-sim — a safe mock insertion teaching the core mechanics (period cover, gradient navigation, a controlled fight).
  4. Witness a transit — a boost-out / drop-home demo at the Hub, teaching why teams "boost light, drop heavy."
  5. The reveal — descend to the Origin Vault; receive first infused gear; understand what ATA is.

Encounters & Beats

Exploration — the Tour. A walk of the ring introduces the sectors as living places (Refinery fuel scarcity, Foundry gear, Reality Log ripples, Commons morale). Teaches the facility vocabulary the campaign's macro-progression (Base-Upgrades) will build on.

Puzzle / Training — the Simulation. A safe mock insertion in a sim chamber: a small period-accurate scenario (e.g., "acquire the item, keep your cover, exfil") that teaches investigation, period-appropriate behavior, and the consequences of blowing cover — with zero real-timeline risk.

Combat — the Sim Skirmish. A controlled fight against a holographic adversary. Teaches the combat system (initiative, HP/FP, statuses) and — pointedly — that a straight fight isn't always the answer. Deaths here are just "sim over, run it again."

The Reveal — the Origin Vault. The descent to the meteor chamber. The warm, trajectory-less, softly-radiant shard in its crater. A senior agent (Chief) delivers the gravity: this is the anchor of all time travel, there is an enemy who works from a mirror shard in the future, and everything history remembers can be re-written. Foreshadow — don't fully explain — the deeper truths (4D fragmentation, what the meteor is); those are later-arc reveals.

Open Design Questions

  • Sequencing (resolved). The Origin Vault runs immediately after Arc 00 and before Arc 01. Arc-00 now ends with the team's escape-by-transit to Atlantis, so their arrival in the deep-past facility is the campaign's home-base reveal; the Origin Vault is the guided onboarding descent that immediately follows — their first proper tour of the facility, ending before the Origin shard. Arc-01's prerequisite has been updated to match ("have already been to Atlantis").
  • How much system-teaching to bake in vs leave to the GM / rules doc?
  • Is it a "mission" or a framed montage? A guided tour risks being passive — how much player agency (choices in the sim, questions to Chief) keeps it active?
  • The training-sim scenario — what era/objective best previews the campaign without spoiling a real mission?
  • How much of the Origin's true nature to hint at the reveal, given the deep truths are late-game.
