Atlantis

The city built around the wound.

A Built Site, Not a Civilization

There were no "native Atlanteans." The Mediterranean civilization remembered through Plato's Timaeus and Critias never existed as Plato described it. The Plato myth is agency-fabricated cover (see The Plato Myth below).

What did exist: in the centuries following the meteor's impact (~4000 BCE), time-travelers from the agency's future came back to the impact era to claim the meteor and build a facility around it. They constructed the dome, the rings, the train, and the infused-metal foundry across roughly two millennia of Bronze Age work, posing as a coastal civilization for cover. The surface population around the crater — facility staff, support workers, Bronze Age locals recruited into the project — was the agency's earliest era of operations, not a separate people. They lived openly enough that rumors of their unusual technology and unusual longevity leaked across the Mediterranean. The Plato myth is what eventually grew out of those leaks, deliberately curated by later agency operations to obscure rather than reveal the truth.

When the surface presence ended (the Disruption Event, ~1200 BCE — see ../GM-Secrets/Atlantis-Fall.md), the project went fully underwater and underground. Modern ATA descends from that continuous-but-reduced agency lineage, plus the additional infusion of Founders who came back from the further future during the Industrial Recovery era (~1800–1950 CE).

The Site

The meteor impacted in reasonably shallow water in the eastern Mediterranean — deep enough that the crater is invisible from the surface and inaccessible to casual maritime traffic, shallow enough that:

  • Modern scuba divers can survive at depth without extreme-pressure gear
  • The dome does not need to withstand abyssal pressures
  • The facility's airlocks operate within manageable engineering parameters

The crater is a natural defensive moat. Anyone who knows about Atlantis and wants to reach it has to do so via the dock systems ATA controls.

Present Day

The site exists. The crater exists. The meteor exists. What the public knows about any of it is a different matter.

The Atlantean Transit Authority (../Factions/ATA.md) governs the site as a "marine research preserve" through a chain of front organizations and treaty carve-outs. Beneath the cover, ATA operations are run from inside the crater itself, in pressurized facilities the modern world has no idea exist.

The agency's name comes from the meteor's geographic location, not from a native civilization. The "Atlantean" descriptor is a site-based identifier — used because the impact site became the Atlantis of myth — not a cultural inheritance.

What the Crater Facility Does

Three intertwined functions, all dependent on direct meteor access:

  1. Gradient anchor. The Origin meteor itself is the projector. All ATA temporal operations originate from or terminate at the crater. Boost and drop transits both arrive in dedicated chambers built around the meteor.
  2. Fuel refinery. The meteor is the source of refined charges used for individual transits, and of orichalcum powder used in the foundry. Refining is slow, careful, and tightly metered — see Orichalcum.md.
  3. The Foundry. ATA's metallurgical complex, dedicated to producing infused equipment. The foundry's mark is the rate-limiter on what gear ATA can field in distant eras. Upgrading the foundry is a long-arc campaign asset — see Orichalcum.md for the tech tree.

A fourth function — the Chronograph — sits above the meteor on the crater floor. See Chronograph.md.

For the physical layout of the facility itself — the three rings, the hex-glass dome, the 16-sector design, the bootstrap-paradox construction history — see Atlantis-Facility.md.

The Plato Myth

By the time Plato wrote the Timaeus and Critias (~360 BCE), the surface-cover era of Atlantis had been over for 800+ years. Rumors persisted in Egyptian temple records, in fragmentary Mediterranean trader oral history, in Phoenician maritime folklore. The leaks were inconvenient — they pointed toward a real location, a real anomaly, a recoverable truth.

ATA's response was to shape the leaks rather than suppress them. Egyptian priests at Saïs received deliberately seeded accounts of "the lost continent" framed as legend, with three deliberate misdirections:

  • Wrong location. Placed beyond the Pillars of Heracles (outside the Mediterranean), pointing investigators away from the real crater.
  • Wrong timeline. Placed 9000 years before Solon's era — impossibly deep prehistory, before any plausible civilization could have existed at that scale. Any historian doing the math dismisses it as legend.
  • Wrong moral framing. A civilization destroyed by hubris and the wrath of the gods. This shapes any modern recovery of fragmentary truth as "lost-civilization romance" rather than "active operational site."

Solon heard the seeded account, brought it home; Plato eventually wrote it up. The myth absorbed the leaks and reframed them as fable. Modern Atlantis hunters who follow Plato's coordinates find nothing — as intended. The seeded myth is a permanent containment operation, periodically refreshed across subsequent eras (medieval mappa mundi placements, Renaissance esoteric tradition, 19th-century occult revival).
