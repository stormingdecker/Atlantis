Chief

Senior ATA leadership. Founder of modern ATA. Veteran of a future that no longer exists.

Cover Identity

Currently: a multi-millionaire retired from the tech industry, late 50s in apparent age, eccentric, semi-reclusive. Owns scattered properties (a modest estate in northern California, a Cyprus villa, a Manhattan apartment, a Mediterranean yacht), employs specialized staff who do not ask too many questions. The cover places him in early-2000s Silicon Valley success — an early-stage investor in companies that "did well enough" to retire on, a man who can afford to spend his time on personal projects and disappear for weeks without anyone noticing.

This is his current cover. He has rotated through six to eight previous cover identities since arriving in the modern era — railroad financier (1890s), aerospace consultant (1950s), early-internet pioneer (1990s), and others. Each cover lasts ~20–30 years before he ages out of plausibility and constructs a new one.

True Identity

From the 2200s, specifically the 2260s–2280s CE — the Russian-Chinese hegemony era (see ../Factions/EPOCH.md for the hegemony's substance). He grew up inside the EPOCH-era hegemony, watched it consolidate, and joined a small underground resistance movement opposed to its authoritarian governance. The resistance was eventually broken; Chief and a handful of others escaped via backward operations into deep historical eras and never returned.

His real name is classified to all but two or three people at ATA central command, and even he no longer uses it. After 150+ years of being Chief, he is Chief. The man whose birth name and pre-recruitment life are filed away in a sealed archive is, in any meaningful sense, gone. (See ../Core-Setting/Aliases-and-Identity.md for the agency-wide alias convention.)

He came back through time to the late 19th / early 20th century CE during the agency's Industrial Recovery era, helped found modern ATA in its current institutional form, and has been a senior leader since. By 2026 CE he has been at the agency for ~130 subjective years.

Physical Modifications

  • Far-jumper modification. Surgical and biological alterations that extend his personal effective gradient — he can boost transit farther up either gradient than standard agents. This is Chief's expression of the Far Traveler advantage: reaching the present from a far-future origin is his long climb, and it dominates his build the way it dominates Helen's — heavy investment in reach leaves less for everything else, which is one reason Chief operates as leadership rather than a frontline combatant. The mod was originally developed by 2200s EPOCH biotech for long-range backward operations; the resistance movement acquired the technique and gave it to its members before deploying them. Chief kept the modification after defecting. Operational cost: significant. His permanent presence at modern Atlantis is partly because further long-range transits are no longer affordable on a routine basis — his charge budget is largely consumed by the original trip and maintenance.
  • Implant suite. Neural-jack hacking implant (interfaces with computer systems via close-range wireless), eye HUD (displays information overlaid on his vision), reflex augmentation, sub-vocalization comms. The implants are not visible at casual inspection but produce a tell: when he actively uses certain skills, his eyes flash faintly with displayed information. Visible in dim light at close range. Trained observers (other infused agents, EPOCH counter-intelligence) recognize the tell immediately.
  • Slowed aging. Standard infusion-related slow-aging plus residual 2200s longevity treatments. Apparent age has been late-40s to early-60s for most of his ~130 subjective years at the agency. Currently presents as mid-50s.

Operational Role

  • Mission leadership. Briefs players. Assigns missions. Reviews after-action reports. The recurring senior-leadership voice players hear most often.
  • Reality Analysis liaison. Chief is one of the few senior agents who personally reads Chronograph output in detail. He often picks which ripples become missions, based on intuitions about EPOCH operational patterns that nobody else fully shares.
  • Strategic counsel. Senior leadership council member. Has direct input into ATA's long-term direction.
  • Rare field deployment. Chief almost never deploys personally. When he does, the players know something exceptional is happening — the stakes are high enough to risk one of the agency's founder-cohort.
  • Mentor. New PCs typically meet Chief at recruitment or shortly after. He becomes a recurring figure throughout their service.

What He Knows

  • EPOCH's 2200s internal structure. He grew up inside it. Detailed knowledge of the hegemony's institutions, leadership figures (or rather, figures who will be its leadership), cultural mores, and operational tempo. This makes him uniquely valuable for predictive analysis of EPOCH's likely backward-projected operations.
  • The other founder-survivors. Three to five other 2200s resistance members came back with him in the 1890s–1910s. He knows where they are, what they're doing, and which of them are still active. Some are leadership figures elsewhere in ATA; some are quietly retired; some are dead.
  • The campaign's true scope. He knows there are likely more shards beyond Origin and Kunlun (he saw resistance-era references to other agencies in his own era). He knows EPOCH has dispatched at least one autonomous-recovery robot in the past (see ../Campaigns/Arc-XX-Stone-God.md). He knows things the rank-and-file does not.
  • What he refuses to discuss. His pre-recruitment life. His birth identity. The names of the other founder-survivors who are still active under cover. Causality-sensitive knowledge of his own original future. The mortal limits of his far-jumper modification.

Tells and Vulnerabilities

  • Eye-light tell. Visible in dim conditions when actively using implant skills. Other agents notice. EPOCH counter-intel would recognize it on close inspection. Chief manages it through careful situational awareness — he doesn't use the implants in front of strangers — but in a stressed operational context, slips happen.
  • Accent drift. His default English is meticulously American-mid-century. Under stress, fatigue, or strong emotion, fragments of 2200s Russian-Chinese hegemonic creole surface — a few phonemes wrong, a sentence-structure that doesn't quite fit. Most people don't catch it. A few do.
  • Implant maintenance. Periodic refurbishment by specialized ATA medical staff (see ../Core-Setting/Atlantis-Facility.md I-6 Genetics Lab and O-6 Medical Center). He is dependent on rare technical expertise that exists in only a handful of agency personnel. Compromising those technicians compromises him.
  • High-value target. If EPOCH ever identifies him as a defector from their own future, he becomes a high-priority target for retroactive elimination. His real identity is therefore one of ATA's most-protected secrets.
  • Slowed but mortal. He ages, slowly. His current cover identity will need to be retired within the next decade. Eventually his accumulated time-debt will catch up. ATA leadership has discussed succession contingencies in private.
  • The future he came from is gone. His resistance comrades are no longer alive in any temporal sense. He cannot return to them, cannot warn his younger self, cannot make different choices for the people he left behind. The melancholy of this is permanent.

The Founder Cohort

Chief is not alone. Three to five other 2200s resistance survivors came back with him in the 1890s–1910s. The exact number, identities, and current statuses are GM-secret material. Sketched possibilities:

  • Operations Director (alias TBD). Runs day-to-day agency operations. Senior to Chief in some matters, junior in others.
  • The Foundry Master (alias TBD). Head of the orichalcum foundry. The person responsible for the foundry-mark curve. Possibly slightly older than Chief in subjective years.
  • A Reality Analyst (alias TBD). Currently working closely with the Cumaean precog. Reads patterns nobody else sees.
  • One who has gone underground or rogue (alias TBD). A founder-survivor who left ATA at some point in the 20th century. Their current activities are unknown to Chief and to the rest of leadership. Possibly defected. Possibly retired. Possibly something else.
  • One who is dead. At least one of the original founder cohort did not survive the long 20th century. Cause of death may or may not be agency-canonical.

GM may flesh out as needed for campaign development. The cohort as a whole is a latent campaign asset — they can be introduced individually as later arcs require senior NPC presence beyond Chief himself.

Encounter Notes (For Players)

If players want to dig into Chief's identity:

  • His official ATA file is a constructed cover. Investigation into it produces a coherent fictional history.
  • The other founder-survivors will deflect questions about him.
  • His tech-retiree cover has been maintained meticulously; surface-level investigation yields nothing.
  • Deep investigation by clever PCs can find the tells — the accent drift, the implant tell, the unusual longevity, the rotated cover identities. Putting these together is a campaign-arc-worthy investigation.
  • Chief is aware the players might investigate him. He plays it carefully. If pressed directly, he can deflect indefinitely. If trust is well established, he may share some truth — perhaps acknowledging the far-future origin without disclosing his real identity.

Pronouns and Presentation

Chief presents as male in his current cover identity. He has presented as male in his previous covers as well, though this is not necessarily reflective of his pre-recruitment identity (which is classified anyway). Pronouns: he/him in modern operational use.

Open Questions

  • His real name. Strong design call: never revealed. The campaign can hint, tease, allude — but the name should remain an unrecoverable secret. This is the cleanest reinforcement of the aliases convention.
  • His relationship to current EPOCH. Does EPOCH suspect a high-value defector exists in modern ATA? Has any EPOCH operation tried to identify Chief? Has he ever personally faced an EPOCH agent who would have known him in the 2200s?
  • What ended his original future. Did the resistance fail? Was it caught? Did Chief's coming back to the past itself alter the future he left, such that his comrades' fates can no longer be checked?
  • The next Chief. Who succeeds him? Is there a designated heir? Does the role pass to one of the other founder-survivors? Or does the agency need to grow a successor from contemporary recruits?
  • Is "Chief" an inherited alias? Per the aliases convention, senior operative roles sometimes pass their codename to a successor. Is this Chief the first to wear the name, or has the alias been used by previous senior leaders across earlier decades? GM-knowledge call.
