The Origin

A time-travel tabletop RPG campaign setting.

Pitch

Players are modern-day recruits pulled into a shadow war fought across human history. Two rival agencies — the Atlantean Transit Authority and EPOCH — wage influence campaigns up and down the timestream from a single anchor point: The Origin, the moment a multi-dimensional meteor struck the prehistoric Mediterranean and seeded the foundations of Atlantis.

Pharaohs, philosophers, oracles, and warlords — most weren't quite who history remembers. They were agents. The Minotaur in the labyrinth, the feathered serpent of the Mesoamericans, jackal-headed Anubis — those weren't gods. They were gene-crafted assets, engineered to fit the myth and tilt the future.

Setting Boundaries

  • No magic. This is a science-and-conspiracy setting. The lack of magic is what forces mystery to resolve into technology, biology, or psionics rather than handwave.
  • Yes psionics. Rare but real. The only non-technological ability category in canon. See Core-Setting/Psionics.md.
  • Single timeline, mutable. History happens once. Interventions propagate consequences forward irrevocably. See Core-Setting/Timeline-Model.md.
  • Cold War analog. ATA vs. EPOCH fight a deliberately limited shadow war — small leverage-point interventions, never all-out, because all-out would risk reality-collapse.
