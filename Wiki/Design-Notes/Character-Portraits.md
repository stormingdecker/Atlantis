# Character Portrait Prompts

Image-generation prompts for embedded agents and bosses. Each prompt is written as a self-contained block, suitable for pasting into Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, DALL·E, or similar. Style guidance at the bottom of each prompt — strip or swap as needed for the model in use.

A consistent house style across the roster will make tokens, cards, and handouts feel like a single campaign. The suggested baseline is **painterly semi-realism, muted palette, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, three-quarter portrait framing, neutral background with era-appropriate texture.** Each prompt below assumes that baseline and only overrides when the character demands it.

## ATA Agents (Player-Side)

### Bast

> Portrait of a slim, agile woman in her late twenties, modern Egyptian (Cairene) features — warm olive skin, sharp cheekbones, dark almond eyes with a faint amused glint, black hair pulled back tight. Dressed in dark practical fieldwear — fitted technical jacket, slim cargo pants, climbing harness clipped at the hip. One hand resting near a holstered sidearm, the other holding a small antique relic she clearly intends to keep. Expression: half-smile, eyes calculating, body language of a professional thief who has never been caught and is mildly insulted you would suggest she could be. Three-quarter framing, neutral background of weathered stone and faint dust motes, warm side lighting from a low sun. Painterly semi-realism, muted earth palette with one accent of turquoise (earring or scarf), dramatic chiaroscuro.

### Chief

> Portrait of a man in his mid-fifties (apparent), tall, lean, expensively dressed — slate grey turtleneck under a charcoal blazer, no tie, minimalist watch. Salt-and-pepper hair cut short, neatly trimmed beard, intelligent grey eyes. The kind of face that reads as a tech CEO at a board meeting. Subtle tell: one eye reflects a faint pinprick of pale blue light from within (cybernetic implant flare), barely visible. Posture composed, hands clasped, expression unreadable — courteous but deeply remote, like someone who has already calculated the outcome of this conversation and three others. Three-quarter framing, blurred backdrop of a glass-walled office at dusk, cool blue lighting from monitors offscreen mixing with warm ambient light. Painterly semi-realism, muted palette, faint future-tech sheen on metal surfaces.

### Doc (Young)

> Portrait of a wiry, intense man in his early thirties, tousled dark hair, sharp features, restless dark eyes that seem to be solving four problems at once. Dressed in 1940s technical wear — rolled shirtsleeves, loosened tie, suspenders, a stained lab apron half-tied at the waist, a wrench tucked through a loop. Hands burn-scarred and stained with grease. Expression: reckless grin, the look of a man about to do something his older self has expressly forbidden. Subtle uncanny quality to the skin — faintly too smooth, as if the surface doesn't quite match the age underneath. Three-quarter framing, background of cluttered workshop with brass instruments and chalkboard equations softly out of focus, warm tungsten lighting. Painterly semi-realism, sepia-warm palette, slight period-photograph grain.

### Doc (Old)

> Portrait of the same man, now apparently in his late seventies — silver hair, weathered face, the restless eyes now calm and deeply tired. Same wiry frame, slightly stooped. Dressed in worn tweed jacket, soft sweater underneath, an analog wristwatch on a frayed leather strap. Hands folded, the burn scars still visible but faded. Expression: quiet, patient, watching the viewer the way a man watches a younger version of himself make a mistake he can't prevent. Same uncanny skin quality — faintly too smooth for the apparent age, the underlying truth bleeding through. Three-quarter framing, background of a dim study lined with old technical books and a single framed 1940s photograph just barely out of focus, soft window light from the left. Painterly semi-realism, muted cool palette, gentle chiaroscuro.

### Major

> Portrait of a man in his early forties, mid-American features, weathered tan, close-cropped sandy hair greying at the temples, clean-shaven, jaw set. Powerful but not bulky build under a plain olive drab field shirt, sleeves rolled to the forearms, dog tags just visible at the collar. Sidearm holstered, hand resting easily on the grip — not a threat, just a habit. Faint scars at the brow and along one cheekbone. Expression: calm, level gaze, the unshakable composure of a senior special-forces operator who has already triaged the situation. Three-quarter framing, blurred background suggestion of an open landscape — could be Afghan ridgeline, could be anywhere — under hard noon light. Painterly semi-realism, dusty desert palette, sharp shadows.

### Sigerson

> Portrait of a tall, gaunt man with sharp aquiline features, late forties apparent age, piercing pale grey eyes, dark hair greying at the temples, clean-shaven. Dressed in a slightly out-of-period dark wool suit — late-Victorian cut, but the fabric is too clean, too well-preserved, as if he stepped out of 1891 yesterday. High collar, dark cravat, a worn leather notebook in one long-fingered hand. Expression: alert, faintly amused, the gaze of a man who has already deduced more about you than you would prefer. Three-quarter framing, background of a fog-softened gaslit street fading into shadow, warm amber lamp light catching one side of the face. Painterly semi-realism, cool palette with warm light accents, deep chiaroscuro, faint atmospheric haze.

### Mercury

> Portrait of a woman in her late thirties apparent age, lean athletic build, short tousled honey-blonde hair, faint freckles across the bridge of her nose, grey-blue eyes with sun lines at the corners. Wearing a worn brown leather flight jacket over a cream cotton shirt, a faded silk scarf knotted loose at the throat, aviator goggles pushed up onto her forehead. Hands strong and capable, one resting on the throttle of an unseen aircraft. Expression: easy half-smile, gaze direct and unshakable — a woman who has flown out of weather that should have killed her and intends to do it again. The face is recognizably Amelia Earhart but the viewer can't quite place why. Three-quarter framing, background of a hangar interior softly out of focus, late-afternoon golden hour light from a hangar door offscreen left. Painterly semi-realism, warm 1930s-poster palette, slight period-illustration quality.

## EPOCH Bosses

### Helen

> Portrait of a stunningly beautiful woman of Bronze Age Aegean origin, apparent age mid-twenties, olive-gold skin, long dark hair in elaborate braids threaded with gold wire and small carnelian beads. Almond eyes, kohl-lined, an unsettling steadiness in the gaze. Dressed in a layered linen chiton dyed deep sea-blue, gold collar piece at the throat, bronze bracelets at both wrists. The beauty is the kind of beauty that has historically caused wars — and she is aware of it, and uses it. Expression: composed, faintly weary, the look of someone who has been a strategic asset for three thousand years and is no longer impressed by anything. Three-quarter framing, background of a sunlit marble colonnade softly out of focus, warm Mediterranean light from the right. Painterly semi-realism, warm gold-and-blue palette, fresco-like texture overlay.

### Rasputin

> Portrait of a tall, gaunt man with an unkempt mane of dark hair shot through with grey, a long thick beard, deep-set pale eyes that catch the light wrong — magnetic, almost feverish, the kind of gaze witnesses described as physically uncomfortable to meet. Apparent age early fifties but indeterminate. Dressed in a long dark Russian peasant rubakha embroidered at the collar, belted at the waist, over loose trousers and worn boots — but the fabric is subtly too clean, too well-kept, an EPOCH operator's discipline overlaying the historical silhouette. A heavy iron crucifix on a leather cord at his chest. One hand half-raised, palm open, the posture of a man about to suggest something you will agree to without quite knowing why. Expression: intent, patient, faintly compassionate — the most dangerous version of his face. Three-quarter framing, background of a candlelit Orthodox interior softly out of focus, warm flickering light from the right, deep shadow on the left half of the face. Painterly semi-realism, deep red-and-black palette with gold candle accents, heavy chiaroscuro, faint icon-painting texture overlay.

## Notes for Consistency

- **Framing:** All portraits use three-quarter framing (head and upper torso) to work as tokens, character cards, and handouts at multiple sizes.
- **Background:** Soft-focus environmental cue rather than full scene — places the character in their era/context without competing for attention.
- **Lighting:** Directional chiaroscuro across the roster. Specific color temperatures vary by era (warm tungsten for 1940s Doc, cool LED for Chief, candlelight for Rasputin, Mediterranean sun for Helen, etc.).
- **Palette:** Each character has one signature color accent (Bast's turquoise, Mercury's leather brown, Rasputin's iron-red, Helen's gold-blue) to make them visually distinct at a glance.
- **Tells:** Where a character has a canonical "tell" (Chief's eye-flash, Doc's uncanny skin), the prompt includes it as a subtle visual cue rather than a hammer.
- **Style anchor:** "Painterly semi-realism" is the through-line. If switching to a different style family (e.g., comic-book ink, pixel art for VTT tokens), rewrite the style line but keep the physical and contextual descriptions intact.
