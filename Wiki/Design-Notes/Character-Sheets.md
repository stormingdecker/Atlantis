# Character Sheets

GURPS 4th Edition character builds for the prologue team and key NPCs. **200 points per PC** (Larger-Than-Life tier per GURPS Lite). Disadvantage cap: -50 points per PC.

These are *first-draft* builds. Tune freely with players in session zero.

## System Quick-Reference

- **3d6 roll-under** against effective skill. Lower is better; 3-4 = critical success, 17-18 = critical failure.
- **Attributes:** ST/DX/IQ/HT. Cost: ST/HT at 10 points/level, DX/IQ at 20 points/level (above or below 10).
- **Secondary characteristics:** HP=ST, Will=IQ, Per=IQ, FP=HT, Basic Speed=(HT+DX)/4, Basic Move=Basic Speed (floor), Dodge=Basic Speed+3 (floor).
- **Damage:** ST-based thrust/swing per the Damage Table.
- **Reaction rolls** on 3d6 with modifiers; higher is better.
- **Skill difficulty:** E (Easy), A (Average), H (Hard), VH (Very Hard). Cost per point determines level above default attribute.
- **Defaults:** untrained skill use at attribute -4 (E), -5 (A), -6 (H), -7 (VH); some skills have no default.

For full mechanics see `../../GURPS/GURPS 4th - Lite.pdf` (32 pages, distilled core rules) and `../../GURPS/GURPS 4th - Basic Set.pdf` (the full reference).

## Pre-Infusion vs. Post-Infusion State

The PCs are sketched **post-infusion** — meaning they have completed Doc's modern infusion protocol and are operational ATA agents. For the **prologue (Arc 00)**, the PCs are *not yet infused* (except Doc, who already is). Pre-infusion stat adjustments:

- Subtract infusion-related advantages: Slow Aging (-15 pts each), Hard to Kill 2 (-4 pts), Resistant to Disease/Poison (-5 pts), Increased FP +2 (-6 pts)
- That returns ~30 points to the budget. For the prologue, GMs can either reduce PC point totals or let those points be unspent (to be allocated post-infusion).

Each character sheet below shows the **post-infusion** build. The "Infusion Package" advantages are flagged so the GM can subtract them for pre-prologue play.

## Build Notes

Rasputin is built as **the EPOCH field-deployable boss encounter**. The Empathic Suggestion psi is the social weapon — single-target, line-of-sight, expensive to maintain (FP cost) but devastating against mundanes and significant against infused. The Reputation disadvantage at -10 pts is a *moderate* tradecraft constraint. **For GMs running encounters:** Rasputin should *feel* dangerous. Narrate the empathic suggestion as a creeping conviction. Narrate the resilience by having him keep getting up after attacks that should have ended him. Narrate the bio-tactile healing by having him pause mid-combat to touch a wounded EPOCH ally and have them rise visibly relieved of pain. The mechanics back all of this; the narration is what makes him a memorable boss rather than just a stat block with high numbers.

## Build Notes

Ember is **IQ-forward** — a genius engineer who is *competent*, not elite, in the field (DX 11). Her power is the Foundry Adept talent (the research engine), her EPOCH-native agent-sense (infiltration no one else can do), and heavy infusion. She is deliberately **not** a combat monster; put her behind Major, not in front of him.

Her disadvantages are the whole point and sit exactly at the -50 cap — do not trim them to make her "safer to play." The Enemy, the Stigma, and the Secret-trigger are what make a defector a defector. If a group finds the dormant-trigger Secret unfun, downgrade it to a flat -5 Secret ("her true history") and spend the freed points on a minor latent psi (Sensitive, 5 pts) — EPOCH's heavy infusion makes latent psi plausible.

# Build Philosophy Notes

These sheets are **first drafts**. Things to validate at session zero:

1. **Disadvantage cap.** I went to -50 across the board. Some players prefer less; reduce if so.
2. **Skill allocation.** I've spent ~75-90 points on skills per character. GURPS lets you go higher; if a player wants more breadth, trim attributes.
3. **Infusion Package handling.** For the pre-prologue starting state, I'd recommend the GM just gives PCs their full 200-point post-infusion sheet but flags the Infusion Package as "not active yet" — they get those advantages online during/after the prologue. Cleaner than recalculating mid-campaign.
4. **The 200-point ceiling for PCs vs. higher for senior NPCs.** Chief at ~350 and Sigerson at ~250 reflects their senior/specialist status. Don't let players try to match.
5. **Bonus character points.** Per GURPS Lite: GM awards 2-3 points per session for good play. With 200-point starting characters, expect 10-15 sessions of advancement over a meaningful campaign before retiring to higher-tier campaigns or rotating roles.
