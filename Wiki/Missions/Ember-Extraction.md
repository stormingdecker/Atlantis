# Coming In From the Cold — the Ember Extraction

> **Status:** Draft. The mid-campaign arc that recovers the EPOCH defector [Ember](../Embedded-Agents/Ember.md) — turning point of the campaign's [gear-up curve](../Artifacts/Anachrotech.md#the-gear-up-spine--arming-for-epoch). A capture-and-extract op where the "artifact" is a person who can't leave on her own.

## Premise

An EPOCH foundry engineer wants out. [Ember](../Embedded-Agents/Ember.md) (born Nadia Kang, ~2190s) discovered — through her own [foundry](../Core-Setting/Orichalcum.md) logs — that a catastrophe the [hegemony's](../Factions/EPOCH.md#the-2200s-hegemony) legitimacy rests on was *manufactured* by EPOCH, and asked one question too far. Flagged for [recompliance processing](../Factions/EPOCH.md#asset-sublimation) (re-conditioning = erasure of the self that asked), she has reached out to ATA rather than be edited back into a loyalist.

She is the hardest kind of asset to pull out: her [device](../Core-Setting/Antikythera-Device.md) is EPOCH-keyed and *tracked*, [Shambhala](../Factions/EPOCH.md#base-of-operations) is beyond ATA's reach, and she reads to any EPOCH operative like a lit torch. So she has engineered her own assignment to a Mark VI foundry-support op on **EPOCH home turf** and signalled ATA a rendezvous. The team must verify she is not a plant, cut her tracked leash, and drop her home as a passenger before EPOCH's recovery team reclaims or erases her.

## Setting

**Default: Harbin, Manchuria, ~1924** — a Russian-built city on Chinese soil, cosmopolitan, spy-thick, and squarely inside EPOCH's [dense East Asian anchor network](../Factions/EPOCH.md#anchor-network). The Russo-Chinese texture rhymes with the hegemony Ember comes from; the era gives espionage flavor and modern-enough tradecraft; the deep EPOCH presence supplies the pressure. Winter — the frozen Songhua River and sub-zero streets are a pillar in their own right, and later a getaway surface. (Tunable — a Cold War setting rhymes with the [1957 "Defector"](../Factions/EPOCH.md#origin-story--the-true-version) cover op; see [Open Questions](#open-design-questions).)

Three linked play surfaces (per [top-down VTT conventions](Overview.md#vtt-map-conventions)):

- **The rendezvous** — a liminal public space (the Harbin railway station concourse, a Kitayskaya Street tea house, the frozen riverfront). *Contact + verification pillar.* Everything happens under both sides' [agent-sense](../Core-Setting/Orichalcum.md#agent-sense) screaming; suppression buys seconds, not cover. 10-ft scale.
- **The EPOCH site** — Ember's foundry-support workstation inside an EPOCH-controlled warehouse/consulate compound. *Heist + puzzle pillar.* This is where her tracked device is cut. Ships in **guarded** and **after-hours** layers.
- **The extraction** — the escape surface: the frozen Songhua, a rail yard, or rooftops. *Combat + chase pillar.* EPOCH's recovery team is closing; the team must reach a drop point and fall up the well with Ember as passenger.

## Objectives

1. **Make contact and verify.** Reach Ember; decide whether to trust her. Her Kunlun signature proves she's genuinely EPOCH — which is exactly what a *planted* defector's would show too. Reality Analysis can partly vet the intel she offers as good faith; certainty is impossible.
2. **Cut the leash.** Neutralize her tracked EPOCH device — jam, spoof, or destroy it (forcing its meteor fragment to escape containment) — *without* tripping Shambhala's Chronograph watch a beat too early.
3. **Recover the proof.** Secure the founding-atrocity evidence she's carrying (a [knowledge artifact](../Artifacts/Overview.md#knowledge-artifacts) in its own right, and a late-campaign story bomb). Optional but high-value.
4. **Extract her home.** Get Ember to a drop point and out as a passenger on a team member's device before pursuit closes.
5. **Deny EPOCH the recapture.** A re-taken or killed defector is preferable to EPOCH than a talking one. Letting them reclaim her is the failure state — worse than a clean loss, because she comes back *loyal and blank*.

## Encounters & Beats

**Contact — under a bonfire.**
- The meet, with both sides' agent-sense blaring. Ember has cut her suppression rig to signal good faith — which means she's *bright*, and so is the team. Every infused operative in the district can feel the gathering.
- **The trap question, live.** Is she genuine? The players decide with incomplete information. Bait: EPOCH may *want* them to think she's a double, feeding the doubt to make ATA burn a real asset.

**Heist — cutting the leash.**
- Infiltrate her EPOCH workstation to neutralize the tracked device. Timing is everything — the moment it goes dark, Shambhala knows. The team is choosing *when* the clock starts.
- Optional: lift the atrocity data from EPOCH's own systems while they're inside — richer proof, higher risk.

**Combat / chase — the recovery team.**
- EPOCH's response is fast and hard (they cannot let her talk). A lieutenant-tier field asset leads — or, for a marquee mid-campaign threat, a [Rasputin](../Embedded-Agents/Rasputin.md) cameo as the closer (he'd read Ember's betrayal as personal). Fight is over the *asset and the record*, on the team's terms (chokepoints, the ice, a moving train) rather than an open firefight.
- The winter itself can force the climax: a whiteout on the frozen river pins both sides.

**The passenger-drop.**
- Ember can't leave on her own — her device is dead. The extraction *is* someone giving her their drop. A beat of trust made literal: an ATA agent keys Ember as their passenger and falls home with the enemy's engineer in tow.

## Failure states (all playable)

- **Re-taken** → recompliance-processed. A future encounter with a *loyal* Ember who does not remember reaching out — a gut-punch recurring antagonist.
- **Killed** → the intel and the foundry leap are lost; the atrocity truth may survive only as a fragment.
- **She was a plant after all** → the "rescue" hands EPOCH a look inside Atlantis, or seeds a sleeper. The [dormant-trigger](../Embedded-Agents/Ember.md#as-a-playable-character) question, weaponized.

## Rewards

- **A new playable character** — [Ember](../Embedded-Agents/Ember.md) joins the team (full [200-pt build](../Design-Notes/Character-Sheets.md#ember-epoch-defector--mid-campaign-playable)), a power spike and an infiltration capability no ATA-born agent has.
- **Research unlocks** — the campaign's biggest: she turns [artifact reverse-engineering](../Artifacts/Anachrotech.md#how-luminous-artifacts-work-as-player-upgrades) from a grind into something achievable, unlocks the **[suppression-sheath tech](../Artifacts/Anachrotech.md#the-glow-is-a-beacon--concealment-is-a-permanent-player-problem)** for carrying glowing artifacts covertly, and closes part of the [Mark IV→VI gap](../Core-Setting/Orichalcum.md#infusion-tech-tree).
- **The story bomb** — the founding-atrocity evidence, a late-campaign reveal that reframes the whole conflict (and tests ATA command if they'd rather bury it).
- **A standing loyalty question** — the doubt is a reward too: it never fully resolves, and that's the point.

## Open Design Questions

- **Era.** Harbin ~1924 is the strong default; a Cold War (Berlin/Vienna) setting rhymes with the [1957 cover op](../Factions/EPOCH.md#origin-story--the-true-version). Which era best fits where the campaign is when the team is ready for this?
- **Trap resolution.** Is she genuinely straight (the doubt is only ever *doubt*), or does the GM commit to a real dormant trigger that fires later? See [Ember open questions](../Embedded-Agents/Ember.md#open-questions).
- **Does a boss show?** A lieutenant keeps it a team-level op; a [Rasputin](../Embedded-Agents/Rasputin.md) closer makes it a marquee set-piece but raises the lethality sharply. Depends on the team's gear level at the time.
- **How public is the leak?** Cutting her device the loud way (forcing the fragment to escape) is faster but leaves an anomaly EPOCH — and mundane history — may notice. A [cover-management](../Artifacts/Overview.md#cover-forgeries) tail could follow.
- **The atrocity's identity.** Pinning *which* manufactured catastrophe she found turns the reward into a concrete late-campaign arc.
