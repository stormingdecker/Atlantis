# Knossos Anteroom, ~1450 BCE

> **Status:** Draft. This is the playable location for [Arc 01 — The Knossos Anomaly](../Campaigns/Arc-01.md) (Acts II–III). See also the [Missions Overview](Overview.md) and the [Minotaur Program](../Myth-Engineering/Minotaur-Program.md).

## Premise

A routine sanitization gone wrong. In ATA's records the [Minotaur Program](../Myth-Engineering/Minotaur-Program.md) is a *closed* operation — the asset was "killed" on schedule by the Theseus operative, millennia ago. The team is sent to a settled past to tidy a loose end.

It isn't settled. **Theseus never arrived. The Minotaur asset is still alive. Someone got there first** — and left EPOCH's fingerprints in ATA's own home turf. This is the mission that lands the arc's second-tier truth: EPOCH can reach into ATA's *completed* history and unwind it.

Crete ~1450 BCE is deep in ATA's exclusive zone and far from Kunlun in both time and space, so the EPOCH presence here is *not* a brawler — it's a rare, heavily [Far-Traveler](../Core-Setting/Temporal-Gradient.md#personal-transit-tolerance--the-far-traveler-advantage)-invested specialist: subtle, careful, and (likely) long gone by the time the team arrives. The threat is the *fact* of them, not a firefight.

## Map & Setting

Two stacked play surfaces (per [top-down VTT conventions](Overview.md#vtt-map-conventions)); the team descends from one to the other.

- **Upper — the Palace District (Anteroom).** Minoan Knossos: fresco-lined corridors, light-wells, ritual chambers, pithoi storerooms, the throne room's antechamber. *Exploration + social/period-cover pillar.* Warm painted stone, gypsum and packed-earth floors, 5-ft grid. Unmistakably Minoan at a glance.
- **Lower — the Labyrinth.** The purpose-built maze beneath the palace. *Puzzle + combat pillar.* Deliberately disorienting: repeating fresco motifs, branching identical corridors, ritual alcoves, and the central den. Ships in **lit** (normal) and **dark** (torches cut, to move unseen past the asset) layers.

## Objectives

1. **Make contact with the local ATA embed.** (They're dead — the mission's first turn.)
2. **Determine what breached the labyrinth** and why the asset still lives.
3. **Locate the Minotaur asset** and **resolve it** — kill, capture, or (GM's call on its cognition) *talk to* it.
4. **Recover evidence of the intruder** — enough to prove EPOCH, and ideally *how they reached this op*.
5. **Extract** before the palace guard or the local political situation turns.

Capture-over-kill is incentivized: a live asset is a biotech sample for the [Genetics Lab](../Core-Setting/Atlantis-Facility.md), and the intruder-evidence is the entire point of the arc. Killing the asset is the easy, low-reward, ripple-risky outcome.

## Encounters & Beats

**Exploration — the Anteroom (Arc-01 Act II).**
- Arrival in period cover pre-arranged by the embed. The cover holds, but the *contact* is gone.
- Find the embed's body — **recently cold**, not extracted. Something killed them in the last days, not millennia ago.
- Investigate: the labyrinth entrance has been opened by an outside party, and there is **period-inappropriate weapon scoring** on the walls — the clue that screams *someone from outside this era was here.*

**Puzzle — the Labyrinth (Arc-01 Act III).**
- Maze navigation: repeating motifs and branch-choices reward mapping, tracking, and reading the frescoes for the "true path" the myth-engineered maze was built around.
- Discover the asset has been **fed recently, by someone who knew how to handle it** — a trail to where the intruder worked. The feeding is the tell: deliberate *preservation*, not accident.

**Combat / Climax — the Central Chamber.**
- The living **Minotaur asset**: a gene-crafted, bull-headed stalker — limited cognition by design, sterile, built for terror (see [Minotaur Program](../Myth-Engineering/Minotaur-Program.md)). A real threat in the dark; the challenge is fighting it *on your terms* (light, chokepoints, the maze) instead of being hunted through it.
- The **choice**: kill it (easy, worst long-term), capture it (arc-shaping — sample + leverage), or talk to it (GM's call on how much it knows about who fed it).
- The **EPOCH intruder**: most likely already extracted (a cheap drop home from this era), leaving the team the chilling *aftermath* rather than a fight. If the GM wants a live encounter, it's a subtle far-traveler who evades and escapes — never a slugfest.

**Reveal (Act IV, at debrief — not on this map):** see [Arc 01 § Act IV](../Campaigns/Arc-01.md#act-iv--extraction--debrief).

## Open Design Questions

- **Minotaur cognition.** How much can it communicate? Its answer to "who fed you?" is the difference between a combat beat and an eerie interrogation.
- **Is the EPOCH operative still present?** Long-gone aftermath (more dread) vs a live evade-and-escape (more action). Recommend: gone, with one *almost*-caught near-miss.
- **The tribute-youths thread.** The Program extracted Athenian youths into the labyrinth "for purposes under review" — are any still here, alive, as a rescue complication?
- **How they got here.** The recovered evidence seeds the arc's lingering question (how did EPOCH know / reach this op?). What exactly is it — an anchor cache? a second dead embed? a note in a hand from the wrong century?
- **Map scale & the maze.** One large labyrinth tile, or swappable sections revealed as they explore (fog-of-war-by-tile)?
