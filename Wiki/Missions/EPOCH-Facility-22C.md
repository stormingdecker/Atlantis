# A 2200s EPOCH Facility

> **Status:** Draft. Stage-4 finale candidate — the "strike at the root" climax. See the [Missions Overview](Overview.md), [The Kunlun Shard](../Core-Setting/The-Kunlun-Shard.md), and [Faction: EPOCH](../Factions/EPOCH.md).

## Premise

EPOCH's headquarters in the mid-2200s, built around the [Kunlun mirror shard](../Core-Setting/The-Kunlun-Shard.md) exactly as ATA built Atlantis around the Origin. This is the enemy's Origin Vault — the anchor of their whole war.

Reaching it means **boosting forward** into the deep future: the hardest, costliest transit direction on the [gradient](../Core-Setting/Temporal-Gradient.md#boost-vs-drop-transit). The strike team must be **maximum [Far-Traveler](../Core-Setting/Temporal-Gradient.md#personal-transit-tolerance--the-far-traveler-advantage) builds** — every point sunk into reach is a point *not* spent on combat and durability, so ATA arrives stretched thin, the reverse of Helen's problem. This is the campaign's **reverse-Mediterranean**: ATA as the outmatched away-team in EPOCH's home era, where every NPC is a watcher, every anchor is contested, and everything is hostile from the moment of insertion. EPOCH, conversely, is *dense* here — near-era operators who spent nothing on reach and hit far harder than the ones the team has faced in the past.

As a finale-boss candidate it gets **full painterly boss art** (per [Overview](Overview.md#boss-sequence-escalation)). The map should feel like arriving at the source of the whole war.

## Map & Setting

A multi-tile facility raid (per [top-down VTT conventions](Overview.md#vtt-map-conventions)), mirroring the [Origin Vault's](Origin-Vault.md) descent structure in a hostile key — perimeter inward to the core.

- **Perimeter — Insertion Zone.** The facility's outer approach in the high, cold Kunlun of the 2200s: sensor fields, hardened gates, drop-pads. *Exploration + infiltration pillar.* Unmistakably future — sleek dark alloy, cold light, 10-ft exterior grid. Ships **lit** and **dark** (blackout-run) layers. Hostile from step one.
- **Interior — Mirror-Shard Vault & Infrastructure.** The functional heart: the [refined-orichalcum](../Core-Setting/Orichalcum.md) foundry, [sublimation](../Core-Setting/Orichalcum.md#epoch-and-orichalcum) bays, and the mirror-shard vault analogous to ATA's own crater chamber. *Puzzle pillar.* Warm shard-glow against cold industry.
- **Command Core.** EPOCH leadership's seat and the anchor controls that project their reach across all history. *Combat + climax pillar.* Full boss-art treatment — heavier texture, richer light, the same 90° top-down rules, only detail density rising.

## Objectives

1. **Insert and survive first contact** as a transit-stretched away-team in EPOCH's densest era.
2. **Reach the mirror-shard vault** through hostile interior infrastructure.
3. **Cripple EPOCH's projection** — sabotage the anchor controls / foundry that let them reach into ATA's [completed history](../Core-Setting/Reality-Log.md).
4. **Confront EPOCH leadership** at the command core — the face behind the whole war.
5. **Exfil, or accept the cost** — a max-Far-Traveler team boosting home is fragile; the ending hinges on how the team spends its last reserves.

Stakes are campaign-ending by design, but the specific victory condition is deliberately left open (below) so the finale can flex to the arc's shape.

## Encounters & Beats

**Exploration — the Insertion (hostile approach).**
- No embed, no fallback, no friendly anchor. The team fights the environment as much as the guards: a future facility built to detect exactly what they are.
- Agent-sense is a liability here — EPOCH's near-era operators read the intruders instantly and converge. The opening game is getting inside before the whole facility knows.

**Puzzle — the Shard Vault.**
- The mirror shard and its foundry are the enemy's engine. The puzzle is *how to hurt it* without being caught in a temporal backlash.
- Read EPOCH's unfamiliar tech and choose between a clean sabotage and a loud overload — grasping that damaging the anchor here ripples across every era EPOCH touches. This is where "strike at the root" becomes literal and consequential.

**Combat — the Command Core (finale boss sequence).**
- The hardest fight in the campaign, against near-era EPOCH operators and leadership on their own ground, while the team runs on depleted Far-Traveler reserves.
- Unlike Helen — soft and evasive — these enemies are built to *win the brawl*. The tension is finishing the objective before the away-team's thin margins give out. Full boss art marks it as the war's last room.

## Open Design Questions

- **The victory condition.** Left deliberately open: *sever the anchor* (EPOCH loses reach — a systemic win), *decapitate leadership* (a personal win), or *both / a costly sacrifice ending*. **Recommend:** frame all three as reachable and let the campaign's prior choices decide which is on the table — don't hard-lock a single canonical ending here.
- **Can the team even get home?** A one-way "strike at the root" sacrifice run vs. a there-and-back raid changes the entire emotional register. **Recommend:** make exfil *possible but not guaranteed*, priced against reserves spent inside.
- **Who leads EPOCH?** The command-core boss needs a face. Is it a person, a converted historical figure, or something stranger the campaign has been foreshadowing? Flag to the arc's overall reveal plan.
- **Staging with Helen.** If [Helen's Stronghold](Helens-Stronghold.md) is the Act II climax, this is the Act III finale (per the [Overview](Overview.md) staging note). Confirm ordering before locking difficulty budgets.
- **What the shard *is*.** How much of the mirror shard's true nature (versus the Origin's) does the finale reveal, given deep-truth pacing? Coordinate with [The Kunlun Shard](../Core-Setting/The-Kunlun-Shard.md) open questions.
