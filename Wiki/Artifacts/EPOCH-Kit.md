# EPOCH Standard Kit — the boss-drop tier

The gear EPOCH agents actually carry. **Not** the [legendary artifacts](Anachrotech.md) you run a whole arc to recover (Excalibur, the Philosopher's Stone) — this is standard 2200s field issue: compact, [Mark VI+](../Core-Setting/Orichalcum.md#infusion-tech-tree), and *better than early-campaign ATA gear*. It's the **level-boss loot drop** of the campaign: beat an EPOCH operative, take their kit, and walk away a little more dangerous.

## Where it sits in the ladder

Three tiers of gear, mapped onto the [rarity palette](../Design-Notes/Period-Equipment.md#rarity--the-printable-card):

| Tier | Source | Rarity frame |
|---|---|---|
| **Period loot** | markets, the fallen, ruins | Common · Uncommon · Rare |
| **EPOCH standard kit** | defeated EPOCH agents | **Rare · Epic (purple)** — *this page* |
| **Legendary artifacts** | dedicated recovery arcs | Legendary (gold) |

EPOCH kit is the reliable, repeatable mid-tier reward — every EPOCH encounter can drop a piece. It closes the early-campaign gear gap without handing players an arc-defining artifact. It is *purple loot*, not *gold*.

## What makes EPOCH kit special (and dangerous to carry)

Five properties, all straight from canon — each is a benefit with a matching catch:

1. **Infused → self-boosting.** EPOCH kit is [orichalcum-infused](../Core-Setting/Orichalcum.md#what-it-does-mechanically) (Kunlun), so it rides the gradient nearly free — unlike the mundane gear ATA agents must leave behind, a looted EPOCH piece is *cheap to bring home*. **Catch:** that same infusion means it **reads as EPOCH to [agent-sense](../Core-Setting/Orichalcum.md#agent-sense).** Carry it and you ping like the enemy — to ATA *and* to any EPOCH operative in range.
2. **Mark VI+ advanced.** Materials, biotech, and computation 150+ years ahead of ATA's Mark IV. A standard EPOCH blade out-cuts the best ATA steel; their underweave stops rounds that would kill through mail. **Catch:** you may not be able to *fully* use it yet — see [Can you even use it?](#can-you-even-use-it).
3. **Cover-disguised.** EPOCH agents operate in history, so their kit is built to *pass* — a sidearm shaped like a period pistol, armor worn as a coat lining. It doesn't glow like an artifact. **Catch:** it passes to *mundane* eyes only; agent-sense and a trained inspection still make it.
4. **EPOCH-trackable.** Like a lost [Antikythera](../Core-Setting/Antikythera-Device.md#capture), EPOCH can often locate its own missing equipment by signature. **Catch:** looting a piece can put a recovery team on your trail — a great follow-up-mission hook.
5. **Often charge-limited.** The flashiest items (energy weapons, med-injectors) run on 2200s power cells or doses ATA *cannot refill early*. **Catch/feature:** this self-balances — a captured energy pistol with twelve shots is a "use it when it counts" prize, not a permanent upgrade. Self-powered ([shard-core](Anachrotech.md#the-one-principle-the-shard-does-everything)) items are the exception — those last.

## The reward loop

Every looted piece pays out **twice**:

- **Use it now** — an immediate power bump (a better blade, concealable armor, a limited energy weapon, a med-injector that saves a downed teammate).
- **Reverse-engineer it** — captured EPOCH gear is [one of ATA's primary research streams](../Factions/EPOCH.md#posture). Turn it in and it advances the [foundry mark](../Core-Setting/Orichalcum.md#infusion-tech-tree) — the campaign's macro-progression. [Ember](../Embedded-Agents/Ember.md#what-she-unlocks--the-research-payoff) dramatically accelerates this once she's aboard.

So players face a real choice: **field it** (personal power, but you ping as EPOCH and can't refill charges) or **bank it** (permanent team-wide foundry progress). Great recurring decision.

### Can you even use it?

A light gate, not a bonding sub-system (we [cut bonding](Anachrotech.md#how-luminous-artifacts-work-as-player-upgrades)): the *simple* EPOCH gear (blades, armor, injectors) is plug-and-play. The *complex* gear (their transit device, encrypted sensors, keyed weapons) is inert or degraded to ATA until the team unlocks it — a Reverse-Engineering roll, a captured EPOCH agent's cooperation, or **Ember**. This makes EPOCH's fanciest drops a *reason to invest in research*, not an instant win.

## The roster

Standard EPOCH field issue. Stats are effect-sketches (tune to Low-Tech/Ultra-Tech); rarity is the drop tier.

### Weapons

- **EPOCH sidearm** *(Epic)* — a compact directed-energy or gauss pistol disguised as a period firearm. Hits well above the era's TL; **~10–15 charges, no ATA refill.** The signature boss-drop.
- **Mark VI blade** *(Rare)* — a monomolecular-edged knife or short sword of translucent-dark alloy. Self-sharpening, cuts above its class, never dulls or breaks. *Permanent* — no charges. The reliable keeper.
- **Needler / tranq injector gun** *(Rare)* — silent, loaded with 2200s toxins or sedatives. Non-lethal takedowns; limited darts.

### Armor & protection

- **Smartweave underlayer** *(Epic)* — flexible Mark VI armor worn *under* period clothing. High DR, fully concealable — the "how did they survive that" piece. Permanent.
- **Reactive coat lining** *(Rare)* — a garment lining that stiffens on impact; modest DR, total cover. Permanent.

### Utility & tech

- **Suppression rig** *(Epic)* — dampens the wearer's [agent-sense](../Core-Setting/Orichalcum.md#agent-sense) signature (the tech behind the artifact [suppression sheaths](Anachrotech.md#the-glow-is-a-beacon--concealment-is-a-permanent-player-problem)). Buys covert minutes against hostile agents. [Ember](../Embedded-Agents/Ember.md) knows it cold. Charge-limited.
- **Chrono-jammer** *(Epic)* — the anchor-/fuel-dampening tech that [delays an enemy's drop-transit escape](../Embedded-Agents/Overview.md#mechanical-notes). Field it to *pin an EPOCH boss in the fight* instead of letting them fall up the well. The single most tactically valuable drop. Charge-limited.
- **EPOCH transit device** *(Epic → research)* — their [Antikythera](../Core-Setting/Antikythera-Device.md) equivalent. **Bricked to ATA** (biometric-locked), so no free rides home — but its shard fragment is raw fuel and the whole unit is prime research. A bank-it, not field-it, drop.
- **Sensor bead / mini-Chronograph** *(Rare)* — reads temporal ripples and agent signatures at short range; encrypted (needs unlocking).
- **Med-injector** *(Rare)* — 2200s combat medicine: an emergency dose that stops a bleed-out or brings a downed agent back to their feet. A few doses, no refill. Everyone wants this one.
- **Comms/translator bead** *(Uncommon)* — instant local-language fluency + secure squad comms. Low-key but always useful.

## Boss-drop guidance

Scale the drop to the encounter — the [EPOCH power curve](../Embedded-Agents/Overview.md#the-epoch-power-curve) means kills are rare and precious early:

- **Undercover embed / field agent** → one Rare piece (a blade, a med-injector, a comms bead).
- **Senior field asset** (a [Rasputin](../Embedded-Agents/Rasputin.md)-tier operative) → an Epic piece + a Rare, and possibly a chrono-jammer or transit device.
- **The catch travels with the loot:** taking a piece may bring an [EPOCH recovery team](#what-makes-epoch-kit-special-and-dangerous-to-carry) — bake that into the *next* session.

## Open questions

- **Charge economy.** Track energy-weapon charges precisely, or abstract as "a few uses, GM's call when it runs dry"? (Recommend abstract.)
- **The tracking risk — always on, or per-item?** Is *every* looted EPOCH piece a beacon that can summon a recovery team, or only the high-value ones (device, chrono-jammer)? (Recommend: high-value only, so common drops stay clean rewards.)
- **Does fielding EPOCH kit hurt ATA cover with its own side?** An agent bristling with enemy tech that pings EPOCH to agent-sense — does ATA command object? A fun in-house tension.
- **Selling the choice.** How strongly should the game push "bank it for foundry progress" vs. "field it"? A conversion rate (turn in X pieces → foundry half-mark) makes the trade concrete.
