# The Anubis Initiative

Egyptian myth-engineering operation. Faction attribution: contested.

## Operational Premise

Pre-dynastic and early-dynastic Egypt was a high-leverage era — a civilization stabilizing around a religious framework that would last three thousand years. Whoever shaped that framework was buying *durable* influence.

The Anubis Initiative deployed a small cadre of gene-crafted operatives with canine cranial features — heightened olfactory acuity, distinctive bipedal silhouette, visibly inhuman. They were embedded as priests and judges of the dead, presiding over mummification rites and afterlife adjudication.

## Strategic Effect

- Cemented an entire civilization's funerary economy around a single recurring iconography.
- Gave field operatives unrestricted access to royal tombs and burial regalia — extraction logistics for objects that needed to "disappear into history."
- Generated cultural reverence for the canine-headed silhouette that would persist long after the asset line was retired.

## The Assets

- Unlike the Minotaur, Anubis-class assets were *intelligent*. Full language, ritual literacy, political awareness.
- Often deployed in pairs (a presiding "Anubis" and a successor in training).
- Lived among humans openly, accepted as divine.
- Lineage technically extinct in the modern day — but rumors persist of one surviving line, deep in the Sahara, that did not accept retirement.

## Open Questions

- Faction attribution — ATA (most likely) or an early EPOCH precursor operation that ATA later claimed credit for?
- The "unretired" survivors — myth, propaganda, or a real loose-end the GM can pull on?
- Were the Egyptian *other* animal-headed gods (Thoth, Sobek, Horus, Bastet) all from the same gene-engineering program, or independent operations from different agencies?
