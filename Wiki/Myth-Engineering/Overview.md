# Myth-Engineering

The agencies' biological influence program.

## Premise

Both agencies (and possibly others) discovered early that a sufficiently strange creature, encountered in the right moment by the right witness, becomes *myth*. Myth becomes religion. Religion shapes civilization for millennia.

So they engineer the witnesses.

Genetic engineering technology comes *from the future* — modern-era ATA and EPOCH biotech far beyond what any ancient culture could produce. Assets are designed in the present, gestated, and inserted into the target era to play the role of god, monster, oracle, or hero.

## The Two Engineering Tracks

Myth-engineering has matured into two distinct technical programs:

### 1. Physical Engineering

The original and most common track. Asset's body is modified for visual impact, role-appropriate capability, and operational survival:

- **Skeletal modification** — bull-headed Minotaur, jackal-headed Anubis, feathered serpents.
- **Heightened senses** — olfactory, visual, auditory amplification depending on role.
- **Lifespan tuning** — assets often designed with ~30-year operational lifespans aligned to mission windows.
- **Sterility** — most assets are sterile by design to prevent uncontrolled gene-line propagation.
- **Engineered ferocity or docility** — temperament tuned to operational need.

See [Minotaur-Program.md](Minotaur-Program.md) for an example of a heavily physical-engineered asset.

### 2. Psi Engineering

A more recent and harder-to-execute track. Latent psionic potential is induced or amplified in carefully selected human subjects, producing assets capable of inhabiting roles that *culturally require* supernatural-seeming abilities. See [Psi Engineering](#psi-engineering-detail) below for detail.

Many assets blend both tracks — physically distinctive AND psi-active. The [Anubis Initiative](Anubis-Initiative.md), for example, gave its operatives canine cranial features (physical) plus heightened empathy and minor telepathy (psi) for their judicial role.

## Psi Engineering Detail

Per [../Core-Setting/Psionics.md](../Core-Setting/Psionics.md), psi potential is *innate* — most humans have none, a small percentage have latent capability, and even fewer have active capability. Meteor exposure accelerates activation of latent capability but does not create it where none existed.

**Genetic engineering can induce psi capability in subjects who would not otherwise have it.** This is hard. It works only on certain genetic profiles (a "right kind of person" prerequisite — exact markers are an ongoing research problem in both agencies). It requires sustained meteor exposure during gestation and early development. It produces psi-active subjects, but often with reduced lifespan, neurological fragility, or restricted power range compared to natural psis.

The agencies use psi-engineering primarily for **role-locked assets** — Oracles, Sphinxes, soothsayers — where the cultural role *requires* psi-flavored ability and a natural psi cannot be reliably recruited into the role. Each program is small, expensive, and tightly controlled.

## Ethical Posture

Both agencies treat myth-engineering as routine operational practice but with different attitudes:

- **ATA** generally engineers assets ethically (informed-consent-equivalent, designed for asset welfare within mission parameters, dignified retirement protocols). They have made exceptions, and those exceptions tend to become campaign-shaping problems.
- **EPOCH** treats assets as disposable. Most EPOCH-engineered creatures live short, painful lives.

This is one of the cleanest "good vs. evil" framings in the setting — both agencies are morally compromised, but EPOCH's treatment of myth-assets is genuinely brutal in ways ATA's is not.

## The Tech Asymmetry

EPOCH's myth-engineering capability is dramatically more advanced than ATA's, because EPOCH operates from the 2200s. Their genetic engineering, embryology, gene-induction techniques, and asset training protocols are generations ahead of anything ATA can field in 2026 CE.

In practice this means:

- **EPOCH can engineer assets ATA literally cannot replicate.** Capabilities like simultaneous physical-and-psi modification, sentient-but-loyal asset psychology, or complex multi-stage developmental triggering are routine for EPOCH and impossible for ATA.
- **Captured EPOCH biotech is one of ATA's primary advanced-research streams.** When an ATA team recovers an EPOCH myth-asset alive (or even a fresh corpse), the recovery is dissected by ATA's Bio-Anomaly Division — housed in the [Atlantis facility's Genetics Lab](../Core-Setting/Atlantis-Facility.md) (I-6) — and frequently produces ATA's next-generation engineering capabilities a decade or two later.
- **The asymmetry was less stark in deep antiquity.** Early-program ATA assets (Bronze Age Minotaur, dynastic Anubis) were engineered with what was, at the time, ATA's contemporary capability — primitive by EPOCH standards but reasonable for the era. The asymmetry has widened over the 20th and 21st centuries as ATA's tech has plateaued while EPOCH's continues to mature in the future.

This is a permanent strategic problem for ATA: any captured EPOCH asset they reverse-engineer is, by definition, *behind* whatever EPOCH is currently fielding. ATA is always one generation late.

## Open Questions

- Which engineering capabilities did each agency develop first, and from whom?
- Are the natural-evolutionary-mutant lineages of *escaped* assets a real ongoing population, or extinct?
- Cryptozoology in the modern day — does it sometimes trace to surviving lineages?
- How does the "right kind of person" prerequisite for psi-engineering interact with recruitment? Are agencies *screening human populations* (in our past!) for engineering candidates?
- When a myth-asset's era ends, what is "retirement"? Euthanasia? Release into hiding? Memory-wipe and reintegration?
