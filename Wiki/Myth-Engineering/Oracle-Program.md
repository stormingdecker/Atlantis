# The Oracle Program

Psi-engineered seers placed in the cultural seer-role across multiple civilizations. Faction attribution: contested, possibly joint.

## Operational Premise

Every major ancient civilization had a recognized seer caste — priests and priestesses whose pronouncements influenced state-level decisions for centuries. Whoever installed psi-engineered subjects into those roles bought the ability to feed cryptic guidance directly into the highest councils of antiquity.

The Oracle Program ran psi-induction operations against carefully selected human subjects, producing precognition-active assets who were then placed into local seer institutions. Each Oracle's "natural" cultural role (Delphic priestess, Cumaean Sibyl, Etruscan haruspex equivalent, similar in Egypt and Mesopotamia) gave them institutional authority and cultural latitude to deliver agency-shaped guidance as divine pronouncement.

## Strategic Effect

- **Direct line into state decision-making.** Kings and generals consulted Oracles before war, succession, treaty. Properly placed Oracles could nudge millennia-shaping decisions through a single readings.
- **Cultivated cryptic-prophecy format.** Oracles trained to deliver agency guidance in ambiguous, riddle-shaped speech — both because *real* precognition arrives fragmentary, and because cryptic prophecy protects against accusations of fraud when interpretations diverge.
- **Information collection.** Oracles also functioned as intelligence assets, gathering information from petitioners and reporting upward to agency handlers via cult infrastructure.

## The Assets

- Subjects selected from regional populations during childhood based on screened genetic markers. Both agencies maintained quiet recruitment networks for candidate scouting.
- Induction involved prolonged meteor-fuel exposure during late childhood / early adolescence — risky, often fatal, more often producing partial activation that left the subject permanently impaired but operationally usable.
- Operational lifespan shorter than physical-only assets — typical 15-25 years of effective service before precognitive burnout.
- Oracles were not, in their own minds, *operatives* — most genuinely believed they were divinely gifted. Agency handlers worked through cult intermediaries who passed instructions disguised as religious doctrine. This insulated the assets from cognitive load and made them more authentic in their roles.

## Attribution Contested

Unlike most myth-engineering programs, the Oracle Program has *no clear single-agency attribution*. Evidence in agency archives:

- **ATA records** describe the Oracle Program as an early ATA initiative, possibly originating from the agency's Bronze Age surface-cover era of operations — i.e., the same era that built the facility and seeded the Plato myth (see [../Core-Setting/Atlantis.md](../Core-Setting/Atlantis.md)).
- **EPOCH records** (fragments captured during the 1973 Reading) describe the Oracle Program as EPOCH's earliest cross-time operation, predating modern EPOCH's formal founding.

**Three working hypotheses:**

1. Both agencies are right, and the Oracle Program was *founded jointly* during an earlier era of cooperation that neither modern agency wants to acknowledge.
2. One agency is wrong, and the other has been editing the historical record to claim originator credit.
3. A third party — perhaps a now-defunct earlier agency (see Hypothesis C in [../GM-Secrets/Atlantis-Fall.md](../GM-Secrets/Atlantis-Fall.md), the predecessor-EPOCH framing) — ran the program and both modern agencies have inherited fragments of the infrastructure.

## Active Sites in the Modern Era

The Oracle Program is *not fully retired*. Some sites still operate:

- The Delphic site has been culturally retired but is still under quiet observation.
- A small number of "successor oracles" exist in modern-day religious communities globally, descended from psi-induction lineages and aware of their heritage.
- At least one *captured* operative (an aging Cumaean-lineage precog) has been working with the ATA Reality Analysis Division since the 1980s, providing precognitive augmentation to Chronograph readings.
