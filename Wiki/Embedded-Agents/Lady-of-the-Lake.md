# The Lady of the Lake

EPOCH embed handler in post-Roman Britain — the operative who ran the [god-king](../Factions/EPOCH.md#existential-stakes) play, lost [Excalibur](../Artifacts/Candidates.md#excalibur), and wants it back. The arc's capture-tier antagonist, and the woman who holds the sword's key.

- **Faction:** EPOCH. Long-embed visible operative ([Kunlun](../Core-Setting/The-Kunlun-Shard.md) infusion).
- **Cover:** the Lady of the Lake — the water-spirit who bestows and reclaims the enchanted sword.
- **Role in the arc:** primary opposition in [the Camelot Arc](../Missions/Camelot-Excalibur.md); [Merlin's](Merlin.md) opposite number.

## Why she reads as a water-spirit

- **She "gave Arthur the sword."** She is the EPOCH handler who **installed the keyed god-king** — presenting Excalibur (from its lake-side [anchor](#the-lake)) to the wielder EPOCH chose. The bestowal myth is an installation op.
- **She "takes it back."** "Returning the sword to the lake" is her **reclamation** — recovering a lost [Mark VI](../Core-Setting/Orichalcum.md#infusion-tech-tree) artifact to EPOCH's local anchor before ATA or mundane history can keep it.
- **She dwells in the water.** Her "lake" is an **EPOCH anchor site** (see below); she surfaces from it because that is where she deploys from.
- **She always knows where the blade is.** She is a dense Kunlun signature who can *feel* [Excalibur's shard-glow across the valley](../Artifacts/Anachrotech.md#the-glow-is-a-beacon--concealment-is-a-permanent-player-problem) — so she seems to magically sense the sword. She does.

## The Lake

Working canon: the Lake is an **EPOCH local anchor** — a submerged/hidden facility from which the Lady deploys and to which the sword is meant to return. This turns the arc's climax from a simple exfil into a **raid on an EPOCH anchor** — and answers the old [Candidates open question](../Artifacts/Candidates.md#open-questions) ("is the Lake a place, a facility, or an extraction anchor?" → *a facility*).

## Why she matters to the recovery

She is the linchpin of the [keyed-wielder problem](../Missions/Camelot-Excalibur.md#the-keyed-wielder-problem): as the installing handler, **the Lady plausibly holds Excalibur's biometric key.** That makes her:

- **The obstacle** — she's hunting the blade the whole arc, and she can feel it whenever the team carries it lit.
- **The solution** — *capturing* her (ATA doctrine strongly prefers [live capture](Overview.md#mechanical-notes) of EPOCH embeds) may hand ATA the key that turns a glowing brick into a working sword, plus a biotech/intel jackpot.

## Mechanical notes

- **Capture-tier antagonist**, in the [Helen](Helen.md) mold — dangerous, [agent-sense](../Core-Setting/Orichalcum.md#agent-sense)-detectable (Kunlun signature — the tell that separates her from ATA's [Merlin](Merlin.md)), psi-capable. Per the [EPOCH power curve](Overview.md#the-epoch-power-curve), taking her is a *major* achievement, not an early-arc given.
- **Extraction pressure:** like any embed, she can [fall up the well](Overview.md#mechanical-notes) once engaged — so a capture needs anchor-jamming (an [EPOCH-kit chrono-jammer](../Artifacts/EPOCH-Kit.md), fittingly her own faction's tech) or a very fast engagement.
- **Her assets:** Morgan / Mordred and other court fault-line players may be *hers* — the political muscle that keeps the team from a clean grab.

## Open questions

- **Capture, kill, or bargain?** Capturing her yields the key + intel; killing loses both; a *bargain* (both sides walk, the blade changes hands on terms) is the most interesting and most EPOCH-flavored.
- **Nimue overlap.** Is the "Lady of the Lake" the same figure as the "Nimue" who ends [Merlin](Merlin.md)? Folding them together makes her the arc's throughline villain; splitting them adds a second EPOCH asset.
- **What else is in the anchor?** A raid on the Lake could surface more than the sword — other cached artifacts, EPOCH's Britain ops log, a transit device.
