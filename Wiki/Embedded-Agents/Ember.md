# Ember

EPOCH foundry engineer turned ATA defector. The campaign's mid-game **playable defector** — a power spike, a research unlock, and a walking loyalty question the team never fully resolves.

- **ATA codename:** Ember (assigned on arrival — see [Naming](#naming-and-the-device)).
- **Born:** Nadia Kang, ~2190s, in the Russian-Chinese [hegemony](../Factions/EPOCH.md#the-2200s-hegemony) EPOCH exists to protect.
- **Prior role:** EPOCH **materials & foundry specialist** at [Shambhala](../Factions/EPOCH.md#base-of-operations) — Mark VI alloy fabrication, infusion loading, and agent-sense suppression rigs. Field-qualified (all EPOCH personnel are infused operatives) but a *technician* first, not a boss-tier field asset like [Rasputin](Rasputin.md).
- **Infusion:** [Kunlun](../Core-Setting/The-Kunlun-Shard.md), heavy saturation (EPOCH standard runs denser than ATA's — see [the power curve](Overview.md#the-epoch-power-curve)). **She reads as EPOCH to [agent-sense](../Core-Setting/Orichalcum.md#agent-sense), permanently.** This never changes, and it is the center of everything about her.

## Why a true believer breaks

EPOCH defection is supposed to be *unthinkable* — [genetic and cultural loyalty](../Factions/EPOCH.md#what-it-costs), a lifetime inside a system that visibly works, and behavioral conditioning that is *very good*. Ember was exactly that: a loyal hegemony daughter who believed, with reason, that coordination-as-care had saved humanity from the chaos of the pluralist centuries.

Her work broke her. Maintaining the foundry meant reconciling fabrication and deployment logs across EPOCH's backward operations — and in that cross-referencing she found the shape of something the doctrine had always half-admitted and never spoken plainly ([EPOCH engineered "some" of the consolidations](../Factions/EPOCH.md#the-2200s-hegemony)). The hegemony's founding legitimacy rests on having *solved* the great catastrophes of the 21st century. Ember's logs indicate at least one of those catastrophes was not solved but **manufactured** — seeded deliberately by EPOCH's own backward operations, so the hegemony could arrive as the savior from a disaster it authored. The utopia she'd given her life to was born of a crime it then took credit for preventing.

A true believer can survive learning the system is imperfect. She could not survive learning it was **founded on a deliberate mass-casualty event that her own foundry's outputs helped enable.** She asked one question too far up the chain.

That flagged her for **recompliance processing** — EPOCH's re-conditioning protocol. Not death: erasure of the self that asked. Faced with being edited back into a loyal engineer who would never ask again, she did the one thing the conditioning never accounted for, because it had never needed to: she reached out to the enemy.

Her defection is therefore *both* moral and survival — which is what makes it credible. She is not a convert to ATA's pluralism (she still thinks democracy is a mess). She is a woman who would rather be a traitor with her own mind than a loyalist without it.

## The extraction — "coming in from the cold"

The defection is a **mid-campaign multi-stage arc**, elaborate by necessity: Ember is the hardest kind of asset to pull out.

1. **The signal & the trap question.** Ember can't self-extract — her [device](../Core-Setting/Antikythera-Device.md) is EPOCH-keyed and *tracked*, and Shambhala is out of ATA's reach. So she engineers her own assignment to a Mark VI foundry-support op in an accessible era on **EPOCH home turf** (dense [East Asian anchor network](../Factions/EPOCH.md#anchor-network)) and signals ATA a rendezvous. ATA's first problem: **is this a plant?** Her Kunlun signature proves she is genuinely EPOCH — which is exactly what a planted defector's would also show. Reality Analysis can partly vet the intel she offers as proof-of-good-faith, but certainty is impossible. The team goes in knowing they might be walking into a hook.
2. **Contact under a bonfire.** In-era, Ember reads to any EPOCH operative like a lit torch (heavy infusion, no suppression once she's cut her rig) — and so does the ATA team. The meet happens with both sides' agent-sense screaming. Suppression buys seconds, not cover.
3. **Cut the leash.** Her tracked EPOCH device must be neutralized — jammed, spoofed, or destroyed (its meteor fragment made to escape containment) — without alerting Shambhala's Chronograph watch a beat too early. This is the heist core of the mission.
4. **Extract under fire.** EPOCH's own recovery team races to reclaim or erase her (a captured/killed defector is preferable to a talking one). ATA must drop her home as a passenger on a team member's device before the pursuit closes — the classic "limited window before they fall up the well," inverted: *she* is the loot, and she cannot leave on her own.

Failure states are rich: she's re-taken (and recompliance-processed — a future encounter with a *loyal* Ember who doesn't remember reaching out); she's killed; or she comes home and *is* a plant after all.

The full mission write-up is **[Coming In From the Cold — the Ember Extraction](../Missions/Ember-Extraction.md)**.

## What she unlocks — the research payoff

Once home and provisionally cleared (a trust arc in its own right — see below), Ember is the single biggest **research unlock** in the campaign, because she is a *foundry* engineer, not just an informant:

- **Foundry leap.** She closes a chunk of the [Mark IV→VI gap](../Core-Setting/Orichalcum.md#infusion-tech-tree) directly — not to instant parity, but she turns the slow reverse-engineering of captured EPOCH gear and [luminous artifacts](../Artifacts/Anachrotech.md) from a multi-arc grind into something achievable. **She is the person who makes the artifact [upgrade tree's](../Artifacts/Anachrotech.md#how-luminous-artifacts-work-as-player-upgrades) "reverse-engineer for the whole team" step actually happen.**
- **Suppression-sheath tech.** She knows EPOCH's agent-sense suppression cold — which is the tech behind the [suppression scabbards](../Artifacts/Anachrotech.md#the-glow-is-a-beacon--concealment-is-a-permanent-player-problem) that let the team finally carry glowing artifacts covertly, and better agent concealment generally.
- **Kunlun & Shambhala intel.** Facility layout, EPOCH doctrine, [device](../Core-Setting/Antikythera-Device.md#open-questions) tells, the [sublimation roster](../Factions/EPOCH.md#asset-sublimation). Feeds capture/counter-intel missions.
- **The founding-atrocity truth.** Her real reason for defecting is a **late-campaign story bomb** — evidence that reframes the whole EPOCH conflict (and complicates ATA's own hands, if command decides to *sit on it*). This is knowledge, not gear, and it is dangerous to hold.
- **She can pass.** Her permanent EPOCH signature, plus her own suppression skill, means she can walk among EPOCH embeds reading as one of them — an infiltration capability **no ATA-born agent has**. The scar is the asset.
- **She unlocks EPOCH loot.** The [standard EPOCH kit](../Artifacts/EPOCH-Kit.md) the team strips from beaten agents — encrypted sensors, keyed weapons, charge cells — is often inert or degraded to ATA until she cracks it. Ember turns captured EPOCH gear from a research curio into usable, refillable equipment.

This is why command wants her *extracted*, not killed: like a captured embed, she "accelerates ATA's research stream by years" — except she's willing, and she's a person.

## As a playable character

A mid-campaign PC (drop-in for a new player, or a "your character died / defected-in replacement"), built to the standard [200-point tier](../Design-Notes/Character-Sheets.md). She is *stronger* than a starting ATA agent (heavier infusion, 2200s training) and *more encumbered by drama* than any of them.

**Role:** the team's **tech/infiltration specialist** — foundry & materials engineering, temporal-device work, agent-sense mastery, EPOCH doctrine. Field-capable but not a front-line bruiser.

**Signature traits (mechanical sketch — full build TBD):**
- **Heavy infusion package** — denser than ATA standard; latent/active [psi](../Core-Setting/Psionics.md) likelier; the usual Slow Aging / resilience, scaled up.
- **EPOCH-native agent-sense** — reads Kunlun signatures with unusual acuity; can *pass* as EPOCH to their agent-sense with a suppression roll (a unique social/infiltration advantage).
- **Foundry Genius** — Engineer (Materials)/Metallurgy/Electronics (temporal tech) at high skill; the in-fiction hook for the research unlocks.

**The disadvantages are the character (−50 cap):**
- **Enemy (EPOCH, hunting relentlessly, appears often).** They want her back or dead. The biggest single drama engine.
- **Social Stigma / Distrusted (within ATA).** She reads as the enemy to every agent who meets her. Some never warm up. Reaction penalties with her own side.
- **Secret / possible buried trigger.** Was recompliance conditioning *fully* absent, or is there a dormant compulsion even she doesn't know about? A GM-controlled uncertainty — maybe nothing, maybe a one-time betrayal waiting for a codeword. The player lives with the doubt as much as the party does.
- **Divided loyalty / Guilt.** She betrayed her home, her family's existence, everything she believed. She's *sure* and she's *grieving* at the same time.

## The trust arc

Ember's value to the *table* is that the doubt never fully resolves. Is she genuine? Mostly, probably — but "probably" is the point. Beats to mine:
- Agents refusing to deploy with her; command assigning a watcher.
- A mission where trusting her intel is the only way through — and the party has to *decide* to.
- EPOCH deliberately feeding disinformation *through* the assumption she's a double, to make ATA distrust good intel.
- The dormant-trigger question resurfacing at the worst moment (real, or an EPOCH bluff to make ATA burn its own asset).
- Her arc's emotional core: earning a place among people who can *feel* that she doesn't belong, and choosing them anyway.

## Naming and the device

ATA assigns defectors a fresh alias on arrival, as it does its own recruits (the [First Recovery](../Core-Setting/Antikythera-Device.md#first-issue-and-the-buried-chamber-tradition) engraves an agent's codename on their first device). Ember gets hers not from a buried chamber but issued at the [I-8 Transit Hub](../Core-Setting/Atlantis-Facility.md) once provisionally cleared — an **ATA-keyed Antikythera to replace her destroyed EPOCH one.** The moment she can drop home under her *own* device, keyed to *her* by the agency that took her in, is the quiet emotional beat that says: accepted, on probation, but accepted. The codename **Ember** — a coal that still carries the old fire and can't be fully hidden — is ATA's wry acknowledgment of exactly what she is.

## Open questions

- **Era of the extraction.** Which accessible EPOCH-turf era hosts the defection op? Early-20th-c. East Asia (dense EPOCH presence, modern enough for espionage flavor) is the strong default; a Cold War setting rhymes with the [1957 "Defector"](../Factions/EPOCH.md#origin-story--the-true-version) cover op.
- **The founding atrocity — which one?** A manufactured 2030s–2040s pandemic, an engineered war trigger, or a specific consolidation? Pinning it turns the "story bomb" into a concrete late-campaign reveal (and decides how dirty EPOCH's hands actually are).
- **Is the buried trigger real?** Commit now (there *is* a dormant compulsion) or keep it Schrödinger's until the campaign needs an answer? The latter is more flexible; the former lets it be foreshadowed.
- **Does she stay in the present or get deep-past extracted?** (The [EPOCH.md](../Factions/EPOCH.md#open-questions) question, now answerable for a specific person.)
- **Foundry unlock magnitude.** How much does she close the Mark gap — a clean half-mark, or "she makes reverse-engineering *possible* but each artifact is still its own project"? The latter protects the gear-up pacing.
