# EPOCH

The rival agency. Originating in the 2200s Russian-Chinese hegemony. Operating backward through time into the player team's present and antiquity.

## Identity

EPOCH is a **future agency**. Its founders, its leadership, its operational doctrine, its physical headquarters — all of these exist primarily in the late 2200s. From the players' perspective (operating in 2026 CE), EPOCH appears as a covert presence active in modern intelligence circles since the mid-20th century. The truth is far stranger: every EPOCH agent the players encounter in the modern era is a backward-projection from a civilization that has not yet been born.

EPOCH was formed in the 2200s by the intelligence apparatus of the dominant world bloc of that era — a Russian-Chinese hegemony that consolidated power over the course of the 21st and early 22nd centuries through a sequence of geopolitical shifts (some of them, EPOCH later confirms, *engineered by their own backward operations*). When the [Kunlun Shard](../Core-Setting/The-Kunlun-Shard.md) impacted in 2200 CE, the hegemony's intelligence apparatus was the natural inheritor of the discovery. EPOCH formed around the shard within years.

## Posture

- **Interventionist.** EPOCH agents reshape; ATA agents nudge. Assassination, installation, replacement — all on the table. Doctrine is partly ideological and partly forced by smaller fuel budget — they must maximize impact per charge.
- **Technologically advanced.** EPOCH agents come from 150+ years in the players' future. Their genetic engineering, materials science, biotechnology, weapons, and computational tools are all generations ahead of anything ATA can field. Captured EPOCH equipment is one of ATA's primary sources of advanced research — and one of EPOCH's primary reasons to avoid being captured. Their *standard field kit* (as opposed to legendary artifacts) is the campaign's mid-tier "boss-drop" reward — see [../Artifacts/EPOCH-Kit.md](../Artifacts/EPOCH-Kit.md).
- **Constrained by gradient range.** The Kunlun Shard's gradient cannot reach back further than ~2300 BCE. EPOCH **cannot operate in the deep Bronze Age (4000–2300 BCE)** at all. Any ripple ATA detects in that era is necessarily caused by ATA itself, natural drift, or an unknown third party.
- **Ideologically committed to hegemonic stability.** EPOCH fights to preserve a *specific* future: a planetary hegemony that EPOCH agents grew up inside and broadly consider successful. See [The 2200s Hegemony](#the-2200s-hegemony) below for the substance of what they're defending.
- **Existentially motivated.** Their grandmothers exist in that hegemony's territory. The threat ATA poses isn't abstract — it is the threat of family-line erasure. See [Existential Stakes](#existential-stakes) below.

## The 2200s Hegemony

The society EPOCH is fighting to preserve is real, complex, and internally coherent. Its agents are not cartoonish villains — they are true believers in a system that, by its own internal measures, works.

### What It Is

A planetary hegemony formed across the 21st and early 22nd centuries through a sequence of geopolitical consolidations: Russia and China deepening their alliance, Western institutional decline accelerating (partially under EPOCH's backward-projected operations), the post-WWIII reconstruction settlement of 2087 establishing a single planetary security architecture under Russian-Chinese leadership, and the climate-stabilization treaty of 2103 cementing global coordination as a permanent governance mode.

The hegemony is not a single state. It is a *coordinated bloc* — multiple national governments operating under shared planetary policy, with deep economic integration, harmonized regulation, and a single planetary intelligence apparatus (EPOCH's parent organization). Cultural homogenization is significant but not total; regional traditions persist within a normalized framework.

### What It Provides

From the perspective of a 2200s citizen, the hegemony solves the catastrophes of the 20th and 21st centuries:

- **Climate is stable.** The disaster was averted through coordinated planetary policy that no pluralist system could have implemented in time.
- **Material scarcity is essentially solved.** Universal basic provision is normalized. No 2200s citizen lacks food, housing, healthcare, or basic technology.
- **Pandemics are managed.** The lessons of the 2030s and 2040s plagues are baked into permanent biosurveillance infrastructure.
- **Conflict is suppressed.** No major wars since the 2080s. Standing armies are minimal. The peace is enforced by surveillance and economic interdependence, not by traditional military hegemony.
- **Mass surveillance is normalized.** Most 2200s citizens have never thought of it as a problem — it is as taken-for-granted as 21st-century traffic cameras or credit scores.
- **Prosperity is broadly distributed.** Inequality persists but the floor is far higher than 20th-century baseline. Absolute deprivation is rare.

### What It Costs

From the perspective of an ATA agent visiting in 2200 CE:

- **No free elections.** Leadership is appointed via internal party processes, not democratically chosen.
- **No free press.** Information environment is curated for stability. Independent journalism, in the 21st-century sense, does not exist.
- **No protected dissent.** Political opposition is managed, marginalized, and (when it crosses lines) made materially expensive. Outright force is rarely necessary.
- **No political pluralism.** The hegemony is the only legitimate framework. Alternative systems are studied as historical curiosities.
- **Cultural homogenization is policy.** Regional traditions persist but within tightly normalized bounds.
- **Genetic and cultural loyalty to the hegemony is real.** Generations of selective recruitment, education, and cultural reinforcement have produced a population that doesn't merely accept the system but identifies with it. Defection is rare not because it's punished but because it's unthinkable.

### Why EPOCH Agents Believe

EPOCH agents grew up inside this system. To them, the hegemony's accomplishments are *obviously good* and ATA's pluralism is *obviously broken*. From their reading of 20th-21st-century history, democracy produced:

- Two world wars (1914–1945)
- Failed climate response (1992–2060)
- Pandemic collapses (2030s–2040s)
- Runaway social fragmentation enabled by ungoverned information environments (2010s–2050s)
- A series of regional wars that pluralist governments couldn't prevent because their voters wouldn't agree to coordinated action

The hegemony *fixed* all of these things by suppressing the political mess that made them possible. EPOCH agents are not defending a system they secretly know is bad. They are defending a system that, by their measures, is the working solution to problems pluralism failed to solve.

This is the philosophical core of the conflict: **freedom-as-chaos vs. coordination-as-care**. ATA holds that pluralist mess is the price of freedom and worth paying. EPOCH holds that pluralism's failures were catastrophic and that coordinated governance is a moral upgrade. Both positions have internal logic. Neither is straightforwardly wrong.

Players are ATA. They have a stated side. But the campaign should never make EPOCH look stupid for fighting for theirs.

## Existential Stakes

EPOCH does not fight for ideology alone. EPOCH fights for **its own existence**.

The historical chain that produces the 2200s hegemony is a specific trajectory: certain wars won and lost, certain alliances forged, certain leaders rising and falling at the right moments, certain climate-policy windows met, certain pandemic-response thresholds crossed. If ATA succeeds in altering that trajectory — strengthening democratic resilience at the right moments, preventing the consolidations that produce the hegemony — then EPOCH is never created. Every EPOCH agent ceases to have been born. Their families never existed. The 2200s becomes a different century, one without them.

So EPOCH's mission is twofold and identical at the root:

1. **Defensive:** prevent ATA from disrupting the historical chain that produces the hegemony.
2. **Offensive:** engineer the past so that the hegemonic trajectory is *strengthened* — disrupting democratic-institutional consolidation where possible, accelerating the conditions for coordinated governance.

The agency's most loyal personnel are agents whose grandparents lived through the formation of the hegemony and who therefore have direct, personal stake in its existence. The brutality of EPOCH doctrine on myth-engineered assets is partly a function of this — when you are fighting for the existence of your own grandmother, ethical niceties about engineered minotaurs feel small.

## Recruitment

EPOCH recruits primarily from the 2200s hegemony itself. Agents are born in the 2100s and 2200s, trained in EPOCH facilities at Shambhala, and deployed backward through time to influence earlier eras.

A small minority of EPOCH personnel are **recruited from the past** — captured ATA agents who defect (rare but it happens — a few have come to see the hegemonic philosophy as preferable), modern-era operatives turned by EPOCH, or historical-era individuals (post-2300 BCE) recruited *into* EPOCH and brought forward for training. These "uplift" recruits are particularly valuable because they can pass as natives in eras EPOCH wants to operate in.

The most operationally significant uplift recruit ATA knows about is **Professor James Moriarty**, a Victorian-era natural precog recruited by EPOCH in the 1870s and extracted forward to the 2200s during the 1891 Reichenbach Falls operation (see [../Embedded-Agents/Sigerson.md](../Embedded-Agents/Sigerson.md#moriarty) and [../Campaigns/Arc-00-The-Cryo-Train.md](../Campaigns/Arc-00-The-Cryo-Train.md)). Subjectively ~150 years old, still active as a senior EPOCH precog consultant. ATA suspects his analysis has been behind several anomalously-accurate EPOCH operations across the 20th and 21st centuries.

The pure-future EPOCH agents are distinguishable on close inspection: their accents drift (the Mandarin and Russian they speak have evolved); their medical/biological signatures show enhancements (gene therapy is universal in their era); their cultural references are wrong-shaped (they make jokes about politicians not yet born, miss references that any modern person would catch). Training tries to suppress these tells; it does not fully succeed.

### Asset Sublimation

EPOCH's parallel to ATA's [Reichenbach Protocol](ATA.md#the-reichenbach-protocol). The doctrines emerged simultaneously — both agencies extracted assets at Reichenbach in 1891 — but EPOCH's version is meaningfully more aggressive in scope and means.

**Selection criteria** are looser than ATA's. EPOCH considers a figure a sublimation candidate when:

1. They demonstrate exceptional capability *or* exceptional positional access (a court physician, a royal mistress, a chief minister — operational reach can substitute for raw talent).
2. Their canonical death is *manageable* — meaning EPOCH can either find a sufficiently ambiguous window, *engineer* a convincing death event, or absorb the forensic anomaly of a missing or substituted body.
3. The figure can be *coerced* or *converted* if they will not volunteer. EPOCH's 2200s behavioral conditioning protocols are aggressive; ATA's are not. Reluctant sublimations are processed at Shambhala for as long as the agency's patience holds.
4. The downstream historical consequences of the sublimation are tolerable, *or* preferable. EPOCH frequently sublimates figures specifically because their *removal* shapes history in EPOCH's favor — a reformer pulled out of a regime at the critical moment, a brilliant scientist taken before her work can propagate.

**The doctrinal differences from ATA, summarized.**

- **ATA waits for the canonical death; EPOCH will engineer it.** Convenient accidents, sudden illnesses, "tragic" deaths under suspicious circumstances are EPOCH operational signatures. The historical record is full of these — most are mundane; a meaningful minority are not.
- **ATA preserves the historical record; EPOCH tolerates anomaly.** Where ATA rejects extractions that would leave forensic gaps, EPOCH accepts and even prefers them. A missing body, a substituted corpse, a "vanished without trace" disappearance — these are not problems for EPOCH, they are features. The destabilizing effect on the historical record is sometimes the *point*: a 20th century full of unsolved mysteries is a 20th century slightly less confident in its rational foundations.
- **ATA extracts at the moment of death; EPOCH may extract earlier.** Mid-career sublimations are common in EPOCH practice. The agent's remaining historical lifespan is then *managed* — either with a body double, an engineered fake death some years later, or a quiet "disappeared into obscurity" cover. The figure is operational at EPOCH a decade or two longer than ATA's protocol would permit.
- **ATA recruits the willing; EPOCH recruits the available.** A sublimated EPOCH agent may be coerced into service, conditioned over months at Shambhala, and deployed against operations they would have ideologically opposed in their original life. Defection rates are nonzero but lower than one might expect — the 2200s' behavioral protocols are *very* good.
- **ATA's tradecraft hides the agent; EPOCH's tradecraft hides the agency.** Sublimated EPOCH agents often deploy *under their own historical face*, in eras where the face won't be recognized. The doctrine accepts the small risk of cross-era recognition (a 1700s sublimation deployed in the 1500s is unlikely to be identified by name) in exchange for the operational ease of not having to construct cover identities.

**Two known sublimations of operational significance:**

1. [Moriarty](../Embedded-Agents/Sigerson.md#moriarty) — Victorian-era natural precog, EPOCH-recruited in the 1870s while still alive, extracted at Reichenbach 1891 alongside ATA's parallel Sigerson extraction. Subjectively ~150 years old, still active as a senior EPOCH precog consultant at Shambhala. The Moriarty case is anomalous: he was pre-recruited and cooperative.
2. [Rasputin](../Embedded-Agents/Rasputin.md) — Russian Grade II generalist psi (empathic suggestion, bio-tactile healing, anomalous resilience, tactical precog), EPOCH-recruited approximately 1909 while still operational at the late Imperial court. Extracted at the Yusupov Palace, December 1916, using the Yusupov conspiracy itself as unwitting cover and a substitute body to satisfy the conspirators. Subjectively ~156 years old (~47 years lived pre-extraction + ~109 at Shambhala). **Field-deployable boss-tier asset** — EPOCH's most-feared close-encounter operative. Currently believed to deploy at 2-4 missions per subjective year; ATA's intelligence picture of his operations remains fragmentary.

Together, Moriarty (strategic analyst) and Rasputin (field threat) represent EPOCH's two distinct senior-asset archetypes. Other sublimations of comparable significance are presumed to exist; see [../Embedded-Agents/Candidates.md#epoch-sublimation-candidates](../Embedded-Agents/Candidates.md#epoch-sublimation-candidates) for the speculative roster.

**ATA's intelligence picture of EPOCH sublimations** is patchy. The agency assumes EPOCH has run sublimations at roughly 5-10x ATA's rate (the Protocol being aggressive rather than conservative), which would imply somewhere between 20 and 80 sublimated historical figures currently operational at Shambhala. ATA has confirmed identification of fewer than ten. See [../Embedded-Agents/Candidates.md#extraction-roster](../Embedded-Agents/Candidates.md#extraction-roster) for the speculative roster of EPOCH-attributed disappearances.

**Counter-intelligence tradecraft.** Any historical "unsolved disappearance" deserves a Reality Analysis pass for sublimation indicators: was the figure exceptional? Was the disappearance clean? Was the political timing convenient for an EPOCH-aligned outcome? Most checks return negative — the historical record is full of mundane disappearances — but the positives accumulate into a pattern that has shaped ATA doctrine on which decades and regions to surveil most closely.

## Anchor Network

EPOCH's embed network is densest in East Asia (China, Japan, Korea, Mongolia), Central Asia, and the Tibetan-adjacent Himalayan region — the spatial periphery of Kunlun. Russia and Eastern Europe are moderately anchored, extending the modern hegemony's cultural-political reach backward into our shared history. Western Europe, the Mediterranean, and the Americas are sparsely anchored: every embed established there required a major insertion investment from Kunlun.

See [../Core-Setting/Temporal-Gradient.md](../Core-Setting/Temporal-Gradient.md#anchor-network-density) for the full anchor density breakdown.

This asymmetry means **East Asian campaigns are EPOCH home turf**. ATA teams inserting there pay more spatial cost, encounter denser EPOCH counter-presence, and operate against locals who trust EPOCH-aligned figures more readily. Mediterranean and Western European campaigns reverse the asymmetry.

## Base of Operations

The **Shambhala facility**, built in the 2200s on the Kunlun impact site. Tibetan Bon and Buddhist Shambhala mythology has provided cultural cover for the area for two thousand years — possibly because EPOCH agents seeded the mythology retroactively during operations in our past (see [../Core-Setting/The-Kunlun-Shard.md](../Core-Setting/The-Kunlun-Shard.md)).

## Origin Story — The True Version

- **~2200 CE:** Kunlun Shard impacts. Discovered within years by the Russian-Chinese hegemony's intelligence apparatus.
- **~2210 CE:** Parachronic theory developed (by their already-advanced physics). First test jumps.
- **~2215 CE:** EPOCH formally chartered. Shambhala facility under construction.
- **~2220 CE onward:** Backward operations begin. Agents projected into eras as far back as ~2300 BCE.
- **In our past — variable dates:** EPOCH agents establish operational presence in the modern era (1950s onward), the Renaissance, the Roman Empire, the Hellenistic period, and selected Bronze Age sites within their reach. They seed mythologies, install operatives, contest ATA in every accessible era.
- **In our 1957:** The "defector" arrives in Moscow. Posing as a fleeing ATA temporal physicist, he establishes EPOCH's modern-era cover identity — the "Russian-Chinese joint intelligence project" narrative that ATA central command will believe for the next 70 years. The narrative is a complete fabrication. EPOCH has been in our past since EPOCH formed.

## Open Questions

- The exact configuration of the 2200s Russian-Chinese hegemony — what does it look like internally? Is it a single state, a confederation, an empire?
- What did the early-2200s geopolitical transitions actually look like? Are they events the players (in 2026 CE) might live through or witness?
- Who leads EPOCH in the 2200s? Has the players' present-era ATA ever encountered any senior EPOCH leadership in disguise?
- Defectors — when EPOCH agents defect to ATA, do they stay in our present or do they get extracted to ATA's deep past?
- What happens to a captured EPOCH agent? Interrogation in our era, then what? Imprisonment? Re-conditioning? Trial?
- The 1957 "defector" — is he still alive somewhere? Was he extracted? What does ATA think happened to him?
- How widely is "EPOCH is from the future" knowledge held within ATA? Is this restricted to the highest leadership, or is the rank-and-file kept in the dark? (Probable: kept in the dark, this is part of the late-campaign reveal arc.)
