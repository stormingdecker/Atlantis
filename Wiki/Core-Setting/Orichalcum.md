# Orichalcum

The mythic Atlantean metal — *and* the underlying mechanic that ties together carrying capacity, equipment progression, agent biology, and memory privilege.

## What It Is

**Orichalcum is refined meteor-shard powder, metallurgically integrated into a host material.** The agency's earliest era of operations — the Bronze Age surface-cover civilization later remembered as Atlantis — first discovered that powdered meteor — ground from chips, dust, and shavings of the Origin — could be alloyed into base metals. The resulting composite retains the host metal's structural and mechanical properties, but acquires the meteor's *temporal* properties.

Bulk meteor fuel is too rare and operationally precious to use as raw material. Orichalcum is the practical, scalable form: a tiny amount of powder dramatically alters a much larger mass of host metal.

The classical myths got the appearance right (a coppery-red sheen, faintly luminescent in low light) and the value right (more precious than gold) but missed what it actually *does*.

## What It Does, Mechanically

An object's **boost cost** through the temporal gradient is determined by mass *and* by its degree of orichalcum infusion. The more infused, the easier to climb the well.

| Infusion grade | Boost cost penalty | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| **None** (mundane matter) | Full mass penalty | Local-economy gear; agents acquire on arrival |
| **Trace** (surface plating, <0.1% mass) | ~80% penalty | Concealed tools; emergency kit |
| **Standard** (~1% by mass) | ~40% penalty | Standard issue agent gear: weapons, tools, comms |
| **Saturated** (~5% by mass) | ~10% penalty | Heavy equipment, samples, captured assets |
| **Pure meteor matter** | Negative cost (acts as fuel) | The shards themselves; refined charges |

Drop transit penalties scale the same way, but as noted in [Temporal-Gradient.md](Temporal-Gradient.md), drop is cheap enough that the difference is rarely operationally relevant. Boost is where infusion pays for itself.

This is why the **equipment upgrade loop** exists: an agency that can produce higher-infusion gear can field more equipment in distant eras.

## Infusion Tech Tree

ATA's foundry capability has matured over millennia. Each mark expanded the gear that could be reliably boosted.

| Era | Foundry Mark | Host metals workable | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Atlantean classical (~3500–1500 BCE) | Mark I | Copper, bronze | The original orichalcum. Produced by the agency's Bronze Age surface-cover era of operations (no native civilization — see [Atlantis.md](Atlantis.md)). Inefficient by modern standards. Most ancient orichalcum artifacts are this. |
| Iron Age salvage (~1200 BCE – 500 CE) | Mark II | Iron, low-grade steel | Post-Disruption (see [../GM-Secrets/Atlantis-Fall.md](../GM-Secrets/Atlantis-Fall.md)). ATA's reduced-operations era. Lower yield, partially recovered foundry technique. |
| Industrial recovery (~1800–1950 CE) | Mark III | Carbon steel, alloy steels | First full reconstruction of pre-Disruption foundry capability, after the arrival of [Chief's founder cohort](../Embedded-Agents/Chief.md). Modern era's gear baseline. |
| Modern ATA (1950–present) | Mark IV | Stainless, titanium, composites, ceramics | Standard-issue agent gear. Polymer carriers for non-metallic infusion. |
| Modern ATA (R&D) | Mark V | Bio-compatible matrices, neural-interface alloys | Experimental. Some prototypes deployed. The bleeding edge ATA can currently field. |
| EPOCH (2200s) | Mark VI+ | Whatever they want | Generations ahead. Captured EPOCH gear is one of ATA's primary research streams. |

**Base upgrade loop:** Atlantis's foundry is a long-arc campaign asset. Player operations that recover lost Atlantean tablets, capture EPOCH gear, or stabilize political conditions for new foundry construction *expand the mark available to all future missions*. This is the campaign's macro-progression layer.

## Infused Agents

The same principle scales down to biology.

> Prolonged, controlled biological infusion of orichalcum powder saturates an agent's tissues with meteor matter at trace concentration. The infused agent's mass *acts* as orichalcum during transit — they boost cheaper, they carry their own memory-privilege envelope, and they can survive the gradient's edge cases that would kill a mundane human.

This is why **agents are agents** and ordinary people are not. Without infusion:

- The boost cost of an uninfused human body is prohibitive even at short range.
- An uninfused traveler is not memory-privileged — they would arrive in the past, but if the timeline shifted around them, *they would shift with it* and have no memory of the prior state.
- The gradient's transit conditions (temporal turbulence, dimensional shear) cause neurological damage and sometimes fatality in uninfused subjects.

### Infusion Protocol

The modern protocol was developed by [Doc](../Embedded-Agents/Doc.md) during the agency's mid-20th-century expansion, using a retroviral acceleration technique she invented (and then carried backward through time, making the protocol a small-scale bootstrap paradox). Before Doc's contribution, the agency's legacy infusion technique was a years-long process with >90% washout. Modern recruitment numbers are entirely a function of her work.

Recruitment includes a multi-month infusion regimen:

- **Phase 1 — Acclimation.** Trace meteor exposure (proximity to refined fuel) for several weeks. Most washouts happen here — some candidates respond with severe immune reaction or psychological symptoms.
- **Phase 2 — Loading.** Doc's retroviral component actively pulls refined orichalcum powder into tissue saturation. Cuts loading from years to roughly three months. Tightly monitored. *This is the agency-defining innovation.*
- **Phase 3 — Stabilization.** The agent's biology equilibrates with the meteor matter. Once stable, the infusion is essentially permanent — concentration declines slowly over years if exposure ends, but never to zero.

Side effects, well-documented:

- **Faint luminescence** under low light, particularly at the wrists, behind the eyes, and along the spine. Visible enough that infused agents avoid being seen by mundane observers in darkness without preparation.
- **Trace radiance signature** detectable by other infused agents at short range. Agents can *feel* other agents in a room. See [Agent-Sense](#agent-sense) below.
- **Slow aging.** Infused agents live longer and age more gradually than population baseline. Most senior ATA leadership is centuries old in subjective time; their cohort is small and tight-knit.
- **Latent psi activation.** Infusion accelerates the same psi-activation process that proximity to bulk meteor does. See [Psionics.md](Psionics.md). Long-service agents are statistically far more likely to develop psi than the general population.
- **Memory privilege.** Infused agents carry their own privilege envelope — they remember prior timeline states regardless of physical proximity to bulk fuel. See [Timeline-Model.md](Timeline-Model.md).

### Recruitment as Bottleneck

Infusion is the rate-limiting step on agency growth. Both ATA and EPOCH would *love* to deploy more agents. They can't, because:

- Only certain genetic profiles tolerate infusion without fatal complications (rough estimate: 1 in 10,000 candidates).
- Refined orichalcum powder is metabolically expensive to produce.
- Phase 2 cannot be safely accelerated.

This is one of the reasons embed agents are so precious — they represent years of investment.

## Agent-Sense

The single most important social/tactical consequence of infusion: **infused agents can sense one another**.

### How It Works

Every infused agent emits a faint, characteristic radiance signature — a "hum" in the temporal field that another infused agent's saturated tissue picks up subconsciously. It is not seen, heard, or smelled. It is *felt* — a pressure-at-the-base-of-the-skull, a tingling in the wrists, an instinctive certainty that *someone like me is here*.

| Range | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Same room (~5m)** | Strong, unmistakable. You know they're here. You can usually point to where. |
| **Adjacent rooms (~15m)** | Definite presence, direction approximate. "Someone is on the other side of that wall." |
| **Same building (~50m)** | Vague sense of presence. "There's an agent in this building somewhere." |
| **Crowded outdoor (~100m)** | Easily lost in mundane sensory noise; requires deliberate concentration to pick up. |
| **Beyond ~100m** | Generally undetectable without psi assistance. |

### Signature Information

A trained agent can resolve more than mere presence:

- **Origin vs. Kunlun infusion** — different shard sources produce subtly different signatures. ATA agents *feel* different from EPOCH agents at short range. This is the single most reliable counter-intelligence tell.
- **Approximate infusion saturation** — a Trace-grade infusion feels lighter than a Saturated one. Senior agents *feel heavier*. Long-service EPOCH agents in particular have a distinctive, dense signature.
- **Health / stress / wounding** — an injured or psi-burned agent's signature flickers. Sometimes you can tell a fellow agent needs help before they show it.

### Doctrine and Training

Both agencies train agents in two complementary skills:

- **Sensing.** Active concentration to extend range, filter background, resolve detail. Skill rolls at GM discretion.
- **Suppression.** Damping your own signature to delay detection by hostiles. Cannot make you invisible — only delay. A skilled suppressor at the same dinner table as a hostile agent might get 30–60 seconds before being made; without suppression, recognition is instant.

This means **undercover work between infused agents is fundamentally different from human-on-human espionage**. Two infused agents in the same room will *know* about one another within seconds, regardless of disguise. The question is never "do they spot me" but "what do we both do once we've spotted each other?" — escalate, ignore, signal, retreat.

The classic ATA-vs-EPOCH cocktail party trope: two agents recognize each other instantly, then spend the rest of the evening politely orbiting at distance, neither willing to make a scene that would blow both their covers.

### Non-Agent Detection

Infused agents *cannot* sense mundane humans, mundane objects (even infused weapons in another agent's pocket are masked by the carrier's stronger signature), or distant infused matter (a stockpile of orichalcum in a vault registers only at very short range).

They *can* faintly sense:

- **Active psi use** in their vicinity. A psi using telepathy or empathy nearby produces a detectable disturbance.
- **Fresh ripples** at very short range — within hours of an intervention, the residual temporal stress is sense-able to nearby agents.
- **The meteor itself.** Being in the crater facility produces an overwhelming sense of presence that takes new recruits weeks to acclimate to.

## EPOCH and Orichalcum

EPOCH has the same infusion principle, derived from the Kunlun Shard rather than the Origin. Operationally identical with two differences:

1. **Kunlun-derived orichalcum has a slightly different resonance signature** — detectable on a sensitive Chronograph or by trained infused agents in close proximity. This is how ATA confirms a captured suspect is EPOCH-affiliated rather than a defector or a rogue.
2. **EPOCH's foundry tech is Mark VI+** — they can infuse host materials ATA cannot, including living tissue at much higher saturation. Captured EPOCH equipment is *terrifying* on inspection.

- Are there mundane medical applications of micro-dose orichalcum? Does ATA have a black-market pharmaceutical operation?

- The "luminescence" — exploitable as identification? Does EPOCH have technology to suppress it?
- The genetic profile that tolerates infusion — is it inheritable? Are there *families* of agents across generations?
- If orichalcum residue persists in ancient artifacts, can you *detect* embedded ATA history by surveying museums?
- What does it look like physically? Does it sound like anything when struck? Does it taste like anything?
- Can EPOCH-mark orichalcum *poison* an ATA-infused agent who handles it? Are there compatibility issues between Origin and Kunlun infusion?
