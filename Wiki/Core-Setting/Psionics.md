# Psionics

The only non-technological ability category canon in the setting.

## Setting Boundaries

- **No magic.** Not in any era, not in any culture, not as a player option. This is a science-and-conspiracy setting, and the lack of magic is what *forces* mystery to resolve into technology, biology, or psionics rather than handwave.
- **Yes psionics.** Psi is a rare, real, empirically observable phenomenon. The agencies study it, recruit for it, and weaponize it. It is not common in the general population — at most a few thousand active psis on Earth at any given time.

## What Psi Can Do (working set)

The following psi power categories are canon. Specific mechanics will be adapted from GURPS Psionic Powers as we build the system.

| Power | What it does | Operational use |
|---|---|---|
| **Precognition** | Fragmented, cryptic glimpses of future events | Augments Chronograph predictive analysis; warns operatives of imminent danger |
| **Telepathy** | Receive surface thoughts; with effort, deeper thoughts | Interrogation, deception detection, agent-to-agent silent comms |
| **Empathy** | Read emotional state of others | NPC negotiation, infiltration, identifying disguised agents |
| **Psychometry** | Read residual impressions from objects and locations | Crime scene analysis, identifying which historical artifacts have been handled by other agents |
| **Telekinesis** | Move objects at distance with force proportional to skill | Limited combat use; more often subtle manipulation (lockpicks, triggers) |
| **Astral Projection** | Detached observer-mode consciousness travel | Reconnaissance — limited by range and inability to interact |

**Notably absent (working assumption):** pyrokinesis, healing, mind control proper, teleportation. These would either tip the setting toward fantasy or break the gradient/Chronograph framework.

## Psi and the Meteor

Working hypothesis (currently leaning toward canon):

> Latent psionic potential exists in a small percentage of humans naturally. Prolonged exposure to meteor radiance — months to years in close proximity — significantly raises the probability of latent psi *activating* into usable ability. Both agencies recruit for psi potential and accelerate activation through controlled exposure.

This connects psi to the central setting element cleanly: psis are *more common* in agency personnel than in the general population, *because of the meteor*, and the agencies know it.

Implications:

- The longer an agent works for ATA or EPOCH, the more likely they are to develop psi.
- This is a recruitment incentive — joining the agency literally makes you more powerful, eventually.
- It is also a control mechanism — agents who quit lose access to the radiance and their psi may regress.

## Precognition Specifically

Of all psi powers, precognition is the most directly relevant to the core gameplay loop because it interfaces with the [Chronograph](Chronograph.md).

Working rules:

- **Range.** Precognitive flashes typically reach 1–7 days ahead. Skilled psis can occasionally push to 30 days. Beyond that, the future becomes too entangled with too many possible interventions to read.
- **Resolution.** Flashes are *fragmentary*: a sound, an image, a feeling. Never narrative. Interpretation is always the bottleneck.
- **Reliability.** Precognition reads the *most likely* future given current conditions. If interventions occur, the future changes — and the flash that warned of it may turn out to be of an event that no longer happens. This is a *feature*, not a bug; precog is most useful as warning, not as certainty.
- **Interface with Chronograph.** A psi-analyst working *alongside* the Chronograph can project where the next ripple is likely to emerge. The Chronograph reads the past; the psi-analyst extrapolates the near future. Together they give command a workable mission-prediction window.

## Myth-Engineered Psi

Working assumption — flag for full canonization later:

Some myth-engineered creatures were designed with psi capabilities baked in. Candidates:

- **Oracles** (Delphi, Cumaean, etc.) — precognitive psis, possibly engineered to produce reliably cryptic readings that could be interpreted by ATA handlers.
- **Sphinxes** — telepathic, with empathic interrogation as their core operational role.
- **Soothsayers and seers** in various cultures — possibly engineered, possibly natural psis the agencies discovered and recruited (or eliminated).

## Engineered Psi — Confirmed Canon

Psi is **mostly innate** — you are born with the potential or you are not. Meteor exposure can *activate* latent potential but cannot create it where none exists naturally.

**However:** advanced genetic engineering (originating in modern-era ATA and EPOCH biotech) *can* induce psi capability in carefully selected subjects who would not otherwise have developed it.

- Works only on certain genetic profiles — a "right kind of person" prerequisite that both agencies actively research.
- Requires sustained meteor exposure during gestation and early development.
- Produces psi-active subjects, but typically with reduced lifespan, neurological fragility, or restricted power range compared to natural psis.
- Hard, expensive, and tightly controlled. Used primarily for **role-locked assets** — Oracles, Sphinxes, soothsayers — where the cultural role *requires* psi-flavored ability and a natural psi cannot be reliably recruited into the role.

## Open Questions

- Can psi be *blocked* or *jammed*? Anti-psi technology in the setting?
- Are there psis in the general population who are *not* affiliated with either agency? What do the agencies do about them?
- Does the meteor itself have psi-detectable properties — can a strong psi *hear* it?
- Are there historical figures whose unusual capabilities are best explained as natural psi rather than as engineered or recruited assets?
